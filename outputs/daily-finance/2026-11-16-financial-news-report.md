## Key Financial News
- US stocks extended losses Monday as a tech selloff deepened ahead of Nvidia's Tuesday fiscal Q3 earnings report — its first since CEO Jensen Huang's resignation and CFO Colette Kress's appointment as interim CEO; Nvidia shares themselves fell roughly 4%–5% intraday.
- Semiconductor stocks broadly underperformed after the US government tightened restrictions on overseas access to advanced chips, adding a fresh policy overhang on top of existing AI-valuation concerns.
- Reports on Nvidia's leadership diverged: the Financial Times reported ousted CEO Jensen Huang was discussing a possible return, while a CNBC source said the board was not considering bringing him back — the uncertainty added to the stock's volatility heading into earnings.
- Anthropic's IPO is expected to begin trading Thursday November 19 at a reported target valuation of roughly $1.4 trillion; investors were weighing what the listing could mean for sentiment across other AI-linked names.
- Bitcoin slid to its lowest level since July (around $64,000–$65,000), extending a sharp November decline, with crypto-linked equities such as Coinbase and Robinhood also falling.
- The US 10-year Treasury yield eased slightly on the day to around 5.11%, while gold slipped roughly 0.5% to about $3,868/oz and oil fell further (WTI down over 3% to roughly $85.67/bbl), extending recent declines.
- The ASX 200 was little changed in Monday afternoon trade (-0.18% to ~9,065.5) as mining stocks were the main drag, while technology and financial stocks partly offset the weakness; the index remains caught up in the same Nvidia-driven uncertainty affecting Wall Street.

## Investment Implications
- With Nvidia reporting Tuesday and the stock already volatile on conflicting leadership reports, earnings-day moves could be outsized and may set the tone for AI/semiconductor sentiment into the rest of the week.
- The Anthropic IPO (expected Thursday) is a significant new supply event for AI-exposed capital; how it prices and trades could influence flows into incumbent AI names regardless of Nvidia's results.
- Continued softness in oil, gold and crypto alongside equity weakness suggests a broadly risk-off tone Monday rather than a single-sector rotation; confirmation will depend on whether Tuesday's Nvidia reaction stabilizes or extends the selloff.
