- US wholesale inflation (PPI) came in flat for July versus an expected +0.2% rise, with core PPI up just 0.2%, pushing Fed September rate-hike odds down from roughly 55% to under 35% and sending the S&P 500 to a fresh record close.

## Key Financial News

- **US PPI comes in cooler than expected:** July producer prices were flat month-on-month versus a forecast +0.2% gain, with core PPI up just 0.2% (vs. +0.3% expected); year-over-year, headline PPI eased to 4.7% and core to 4.2%. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-august-13-sp-500-record-high-nasdaq-dow-inflation-100145282.html))
- **Fed rate-hike odds fall sharply:** The softer wholesale inflation data pushed the implied probability of a September Fed rate hike from about 55% down to under 35%, per CME FedWatch data, though analysts still expect further increases by year-end. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-august-13-sp-500-record-high-nasdaq-dow-inflation-100145282.html))
- **Wall Street sets fresh records:** The S&P 500 rose 0.65% to a record 7,799 and the Nasdaq Composite added 0.81% to 26,803; the Dow Jones gained a more modest 0.13% to 53,840. Technology and industrial stocks led, while consumer cyclical and communication services names lagged. ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/08/13/stock-market-today-aug-13-stocks-rise-as-inflation-cools-workday-soars-18/))
- **Workday buyout report drives huge rally:** Workday shares surged 18-21% intraday on reports that Silver Lake Partners was exploring an acquisition; Harmonic climbed about 9% after beating earnings and raising guidance. Cisco and Cerebras fell on earnings disappointments, and Amazon slipped amid an ongoing AI-training legal dispute. Applied Materials was due to report after the close. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-august-13-sp-500-record-high-nasdaq-dow-inflation-100145282.html))
- **Jobless claims tick higher:** Initial jobless claims rose to 209,000, above the 202,000 forecast, while continuing claims fell to 1.777 million, slightly better than expected — a mixed but still resilient labour-market read. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-august-13-sp-500-record-high-nasdaq-dow-inflation-100145282.html))
- **ASX 200 falls on materials and telco weakness:** Australia's benchmark dropped 21 points (-0.23%) to 9,188, with 623 decliners outpacing 447 advancers. Telecom Services, Metals & Mining, and Materials sectors led losses; Alcoa Corp DRC fell 7.43% and Austal Ltd dropped 6.77%, while Cleanaway Waste Management jumped 14.98% and ASX Ltd rose 8.81%. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/australia-stocks-lower-at-close-of-trade-spasx-200-down-023-4856667))
- **Oil holds firm despite large inventory build:** Brent crude eased 1.44% to about $90.28/bbl and WTI slipped to roughly $80-82/bbl, even after US commercial crude stockpiles jumped 17.4 million barrels. The EIA estimates 600,000 bpd of Middle East output will stay offline through 2027 due to the Strait of Hormuz disruption, offsetting the bearish inventory data. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/price-of-oil-08-13-2026/); [Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/oil-markets-latam-thursday-august-13-2026/))

## Investment Implications

- A second straight cooler-than-expected inflation print (following Wednesday's CPI) — this time on the wholesale side — has meaningfully reduced near-term Fed hike risk, supporting the rally in US equities to fresh highs, though analysts caution multiple hikes could still arrive by year-end.
- The Workday buyout speculation underscores continued private equity appetite for enterprise software, and could prompt renewed scrutiny of other mid-cap SaaS names as potential takeover targets.
- The divergence between record US equity highs and a softer ASX 200 shows commodity- and rate-sensitive sectors (materials, telcos) remain a drag on Australian markets even as the global inflation backdrop improves.
