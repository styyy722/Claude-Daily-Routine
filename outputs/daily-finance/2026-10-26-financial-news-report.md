## Key Financial News
- **Anthropic's IPO filing reignites the AI trade**: Anthropic publicly filed its S-1 to list as "ANT," reporting $16.3bn in revenue for the six months through June (versus $4.6bn for all of 2025), $9bn in AI-infrastructure spending in H1 (versus $7.3bn for all of 2025), and an $80.7bn net loss through June that the company attributes mostly to accounting charges; about a fifth of revenue came from two customers. The filing also disclosed a compute-spending commitment of up to $120.5bn with SpaceX through 2029 (~$3.1bn/month once capacity is delivered, expected December 2026), and flagged increasingly restrictive AI regulation as a business risk. The debut is expected to rival or surpass SpaceX's June IPO.
- **US equities rally into a pivotal week**: The S&P 500 (+0.70%), Nasdaq (+0.82%) and Dow (+0.28%) all rose, with the Russell 2000 up 0.4% to 2,786.42, as tech led gains on the AI-trade rebound; CoreWeave turned positive despite recent AI-financing worries (neocloud stocks remain down more than 40% since late July per RBC).
- **FOMC decision looms**: The Fed's two-day meeting runs Tuesday–Wednesday with the rate decision due Wednesday 2:00pm ET; markets widely expect a hold, with economists flagging a possible "hawkish pause" under Chair Kevin Warsh following hotter-than-expected inflation data. The ECB, Bank of Canada and Bank of Japan also hold policy meetings this week.
- **RBA's November decision in focus**: The Reserve Bank of Australia's board meets Tuesday 3 November (announcement 2:30pm AEST); the cash rate currently stands at 4.60% following a 25bp hike in September. Bank forecasts are mixed (several expect a hold; at least one forecasts a further hike to 4.85%), with the September-quarter CPI — due late October — flagged as the key input ahead of the decision.
- **Oil diverges on Middle East risk**: Brent crude held near $110–112/bbl — its highest since April — on continued Middle East tensions and reported Houthi attacks on Saudi Arabia, while WTI fell roughly 2% to ~$101.25/bbl; US retail gasoline averaged $4.28/gallon.
- **Big Tech earnings and capex headlines**: Alphabet and Microsoft report Wednesday after the close; Microsoft is offering voluntary buyouts to roughly 60,000 employees, while Meta is planning to raise up to $95bn for AI infrastructure, starting with $35bn in stock. Monday's reporters included Verizon, Welltower, Cadence Design Systems, Nucor, Public Storage and Amkor.
- **Notable single-stock moves and other developments**: Tesla rose about 6% after a Barclays upgrade to Overweight with a $450 price target; Lumentum jumped 10% on a StoneX Buy initiation with a $1,200 target. Brazil's iShares MSCI Brazil ETF (EWZ) fell sharply in trading following Lula's re-election. Goldman Sachs noted retail trading has fallen to about 9% of US equity volume, down from roughly 15% late last year, while RBC's Lori Calvasina flagged renewed faltering among the S&P 500's ten largest stocks.

## Investment Implications
- With the FOMC decision, Alphabet/Microsoft earnings and the RBA meeting all landing within the same week, near-term volatility risk is elevated; Monday's rally reflects renewed AI-trade optimism but remains vulnerable to a hawkish Fed surprise or disappointing mega-cap earnings.
- The divergence between Brent (near multi-month highs on Middle East risk) and WTI (down ~2%) is worth monitoring — a sustained Brent premium tied to Strait of Hormuz/Red Sea shipping risk could still feed into inflation expectations ahead of this week's central bank decisions.
- Anthropic's IPO filing — with its scale of revenue growth alongside an $80.7bn loss and heavy compute commitments — is likely to become a reference point for how markets price AI-infrastructure spending and financing risk across the sector in the coming weeks.
