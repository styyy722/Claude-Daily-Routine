# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-09-04

## Executive Summary
- US stocks closed lower after August nonfarm payrolls surged 162,000 (vs. ~55,000 expected), reviving Fed rate-hike bets and snapping the week's rebound (S&P 500 -0.38%, Dow -0.51%, Nasdaq -0.29%).
- Gold fell over 1% and Treasury yields firmed as markets repriced September Fed odds back toward a hike, reversing some of Fed Governor Waller's earlier dovish signal.
- The ASX 200 edged down 0.16% on weakness in energy and mining stocks (Ampol -5.45%, Viva Energy -3.49%), even as advancers outnumbered decliners, while the US-Iran Strait of Hormuz conflict continued to underpin oil-supply risk.

## Key Financial News
- **August payrolls crush forecasts**: Nonfarm payrolls rose 162,000 in August, well above the ~55,000 consensus, the strongest monthly gain since March; the unemployment rate held at 4.1% and average hourly earnings rose 0.3% m/m (+3.1% y/y). ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/empsit.nr0.htm), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-04/us-employers-add-162-000-jobs-topping-all-estimates))
- **Stocks fall on hawkish repricing**: The strong jobs data paradoxically weighed on equities as traders raised the odds of a September Fed rate hike, reversing dovish sentiment set by Fed Governor Christopher Waller's earlier remarks that he could support holding rates steady if inflation keeps easing. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-4-133716564.html))
- **Gold and yields react to Fed repricing**: Gold dropped roughly 1.4% to around $4,478/oz as safe-haven demand eased and rate-hike odds firmed; the 10-year Treasury yield held near 4.76-4.77%, close to recent multi-year highs.
- **ASX 200 slips on energy/mining weakness**: Australian shares fell 0.16% as Ampol (-5.45%) and Viva Energy (-3.49%) led losses in the energy sector alongside resources and metals & mining names, even though advancing stocks (660) outnumbered decliners (419) on the broader market. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/australia-stocks-lower-at-close-of-trade-spasx-200-down-016-4888857))
- **Corporate movers**: Lululemon shares plummeted after disappointing revenue results and cut guidance, while memory-chip makers Sandisk and Micron advanced on continued strong chip demand despite the broader market pullback. ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/09/04/stock-market-midday-sept-4-stocks-edge-lower-on-strong-jobs-report-as-lululemon-plummets/))
- **Strait of Hormuz risk persists**: The US-Iran conflict around the Strait of Hormuz continued, with the US military escorting commercial tankers (roughly 18 million barrels this period) through the strait following the latest round of strikes earlier in the week, keeping a floor under oil-supply risk even as WTI held roughly flat near $91/bbl. ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/02/politics/latest-us-strikes-against-iran-marked-a-shift-in-strategy))
- **Fed odds swing on the data**: September rate-hike probability estimates moved higher after the payrolls beat, with different trackers showing odds in a roughly 50-59% range immediately following the release, up from the high-40s/low-50s beforehand.

## Investment Implications
- A stronger-than-expected labour market keeps the Fed's inflation mandate in focus ahead of the September 15-16 FOMC meeting, raising the risk that equity markets continue to trade "good news is bad news" on macro data into the decision.
- Persistent Strait of Hormuz tensions keep energy-supply and shipping-cost risk elevated for portfolios exposed to transport, logistics and inflation-sensitive sectors, even on days when oil prices themselves are flat.
- Gold's pullback alongside firmer hike odds highlights how sensitive precious-metals positioning remains to shifts in Fed rate expectations, a dynamic likely to persist until more clarity emerges on the September decision.
