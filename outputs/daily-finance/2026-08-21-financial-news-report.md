# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-08-21

## Executive Summary
- US stocks rebounded Friday (S&P 500 +0.4%, Dow +1%, Nasdaq +0.4%) but all three major indexes still posted weekly losses after a bond-market-driven sell-off earlier in the week.
- Bitcoin jumped over 6% above $77,000, posting its biggest weekly rally in more than two years, after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent signalled further intervention to curb long-duration bond yields.
- The ASX 200 fell 0.27% to 9,058.9 as earlier reporting-season winners reversed, even as gold miners rallied on a 1.04% jump in spot bullion.

## Key Financial News

- **Stocks rebound but post weekly losses:** The S&P 500 rose 0.43% to 7,674.37, the Dow gained 0.98% (+517.80 pts) to 53,277.01, and the Nasdaq Composite added 0.43% to 26,180.45; for the week, the S&P 500 fell 1.4%, the Dow lost 0.8% and the Nasdaq dropped 2.1% after a sharp mid-week bond-market sell-off.
- **Long-dated yields still climbing:** The 10-year Treasury yield rose more than 3 basis points to 4.734% and the 30-year gained more than 3 basis points to 5.273%, extending a volatile week for government debt despite the Treasury's buyback intervention.
- **Bitcoin surges on Treasury intervention hopes:** Bitcoin jumped more than 6% to trade above $77,000, posting a roughly 22% weekly gain — its biggest weekly rally in over two years — after Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent suggested further action to curb rising long-duration yields, which boosted risk appetite broadly.
- **Gold and mining stocks rally:** Spot gold rose 1.04% to around $4,619/oz, supporting a rally in gold mining shares as investors sought safe-haven and inflation-hedge assets amid bond-market volatility.
- **Oil eases slightly:** WTI crude slipped $0.77 to $87.06/barrel, pulling back modestly after a volatile week driven by Strait of Hormuz-related supply concerns.
- **AUD/USD hits multi-week high:** The Australian dollar climbed to around 0.7171, its strongest level in roughly ten weeks, supported by a softer US dollar backdrop.
- **ASX earnings-season winners reverse:** The ASX 200 fell 0.27% to 9,058.9 as some of the week's reporting-season standouts gave back gains, though gold miners bucked the trend on the rally in bullion prices.

## Investment Implications

- The rebound in equities alongside still-rising long yields suggests markets are cautiously optimistic about Treasury intervention rather than convinced the bond-market volatility is fully resolved.
- Bitcoin's outsized rally shows risk assets remain highly sensitive to signals of further Treasury or Fed support, a dynamic that could continue to drive outsized moves in crypto and other high-beta assets.
- Gold's advance alongside a weaker US dollar and multi-week AUD highs points to broader positioning against continued bond-market and fiscal uncertainty rather than a clean "risk-on" rotation.
