## Key Financial News

- **Bank of Japan hikes to a 31-year high**: The BOJ raised its policy rate 25bp to 1.25% on Friday in a 7-2 split vote (dissenters Toichiro Asada and Ayano Sato), citing risk that inflation deviates above its 2% target from H2 FY2026; the yen weakened rather than strengthened, with USD/JPY pushing back above 157 after Governor Kazuo Ueda gave no commitment to further hikes. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-09-18/bank-of-japan-hikes-interest-rates-in-a-split-vote-yen-drops); [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/18/japans-interest-rate-hiked-to-31-year-high-at-1-25-as-inflation-rises))
- **Iran stages "Janfada" military parade**: IRGC-affiliated personnel, including armed women, marched through Tehran on Friday in a state-organised "Sacrifice for Iran" exercise beneath a banner depicting US President Trump in crosshairs, underscoring continued regional escalation risk alongside the broader 2026 Iran war.
- **Saudi pipeline restoration continues**: Saudi Arabia is still working to restore its ~7M bpd East-West pipeline (shut since 11 September after drone attacks), targeting roughly half capacity within days and full operations in about six weeks; US Energy Secretary Chris Wright has said the outage "will be measured in days," a key swing factor for oil this week. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-hormuz-iran-war.html))
- **Gold ticks up on safe-haven demand**: Spot gold rose to roughly $4,377.75/oz on Saturday, up $31.85 (+0.73%) versus Friday's close per 150Currency, consistent with a modest weekend safe-haven bid as Middle East tensions stayed elevated. ([150Currency](https://www.150currency.com/))
- **Wall Street closed a volatile, Fed-hike week mixed**: Friday's session left the S&P 500 +0.17% at 7,650.50 and Nasdaq Composite +0.39% at 26,522.55, while the Dow fell 0.18% to 51,682.64; the US 10-year Treasury yield held near 5.00% after touching a post-2007 high of 5.041% mid-week following this week's first Fed rate hike since 2023.
- **RBA rate-hike odds remain elevated**: Markets continued to price roughly 88-90% odds of an RBA hike at the 29 September meeting, following Governor Michele Bullock's hawkish parliamentary testimony and the Fed's own 25bp increase; the ASX 200 closed Friday essentially flat at 8,731.
- **No fresh iron-ore print over the weekend**: With Asian futures markets also closed, the last confirmed USD/tonne spot level (~$97.42/T, 17 September) remains the most recent reference; it is carried forward here rather than treated as a new figure.

## Investment Implications
- The BOJ's hike to a 31-year high, paired with a weaker yen and no forward guidance from Ueda, signals policy divergence risk that could keep USD/JPY volatile into next week and matters for carry-trade-sensitive assets.
- Continued Iranian military signalling alongside the still-incomplete Saudi pipeline restoration keeps a geopolitical risk premium in oil and safe-haven demand in gold heading into next week, even as prices have eased from their highs.
- With US and Australian cash markets closed all weekend, Monday's reopen will need to digest both the BOJ decision and any further Middle East developments in one move; the RBA's 29 September decision remains the key domestic catalyst to watch.
