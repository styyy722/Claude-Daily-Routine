## Key Financial News

- **Iran strikes ADNOC tanker in Hormuz:** Iran hit an Abu Dhabi National Oil Company tanker with a missile in the Strait of Hormuz in the early hours of Saturday, the 16th such attack since the war began; the UAE called it an act of piracy and, with Qatar, condemned the strike. Casualty reports were conflicting, with ADNOC initially citing none and later accounts describing crew injuries. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/8/8/uae-says-iran-targeted-adnoc-tanker-in-hormuz-no-casualties-2))
- **Hormuz deal "close" but conditions attached:** Iran's Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Tehran is "very close" to an agreement with Oman on a temporary shipping route through the Strait, but reopening remains conditional on compensation for alleged US violations and a proposed ban on US and Israeli vessels transiting the waterway. ([Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/energy/articles/iran-says-hormuz-deal-close-133154802.html))
- **Weak July jobs report reshapes Fed odds:** The US economy lost 23,000 jobs in July against expectations of a gain, with 146,000 in downward revisions to prior months; unemployment ticked down to 4.1%. Fed funds futures cut the odds of a September rate hike to about 40% from roughly 55% before the release. ([Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/eriksherman/2026/08/07/july-jobs-loss-fed-interest-rate-expectations/))
- **Wall Street closes at records:** The S&P 500 rose 0.62% to a record 7,757.64, the Nasdaq Composite climbed 1.3% to 26,690.62, and the Dow added 151.83 points (+0.28%) to 54,036.93, capping the S&P's strongest week since April. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-august-7-nasdaq-dow-sp-500-july-jobs-report-surprises-100009572.html))
- **ASX 200 takes a breather:** Australia's benchmark slipped 0.09% to 9,263.60 on Friday, pausing after a five-day winning streak and back-to-back record highs as investors positioned ahead of next week's RBA decision. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/australia-stocks-lower-at-close-of-trade-spasx-200-down-009-4845143))
- **Oil slumps on de-escalation hopes:** WTI crude rose about 1% to settle at $78.20/bbl Friday but still fell more than 7% for the week as prospects for a Hormuz reopening deal weighed on prices, even as fresh Saturday attacks underscored the conflict is unresolved. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-08-04/oil-slumps-on-prospects-for-hormuz-reopening-deal))
- **Gold approaches record territory:** Spot gold traded near $4,350/oz Friday, climbing late in the week as the weak jobs data reinforced the case for the Fed to hold rather than hike, boosting demand for the rate-sensitive metal. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-friday-august-7-2026-gold-prices-continue-to-rise-even-after-july-jobs-report-misses-122102132.html))

## Investment Implications

- The gap between de-escalation hopes (a Hormuz deal described as "close") and continued attacks (Saturday's tanker strike) leaves oil markets exposed to two-way risk; further declines on deal progress could reverse quickly if talks stall or violence resumes.
- Softer labour data has shifted the Fed narrative from hike risk toward a hold bias, a dynamic that has supported both equities and gold near-term — though the RBA's Statement on Monetary Policy on 11 August could reset expectations closer to home.
- Record US equity closes alongside a firm gold bid suggest markets are pricing resilience despite geopolitical risk; a stalled Hormuz deal or renewed tanker attacks are the key catalysts that could unwind both trades.
