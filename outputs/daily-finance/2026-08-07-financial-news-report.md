# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-08-07

## Executive Summary
- The S&P 500 closed at a record 7,757.64 (+0.62%) after July nonfarm payrolls unexpectedly fell 23,000, cooling expectations for a near-term Fed rate hike.
- September rate-hike odds dropped to roughly 42-44% (from ~57-67% a week earlier), while the probability the Fed holds rates rose to over 60%, per CME FedWatch and LSEG data.
- Oil extended a rally toward US$78/bbl on Strait of Hormuz tensions, lifting ASX energy names, while gold jumped over 2.5% on safe-haven demand; the ASX 200 slipped modestly to 9,263.60 (-0.09%).

## Key Financial News
- US labour market: July nonfarm payrolls fell by 23,000 (vs. expectations of roughly +80,000 added), driven by a 53,000 drop in government jobs and softness in retail, leisure/hospitality and healthcare hiring; unemployment ticked down slightly to 4.1%. ([BLS](https://www.bls.gov/news.release/archives/empsit_08072026.htm))
- US equities rallied on the weak jobs print, with the S&P 500 posting a record close (7,757.64, +0.62%), the Nasdaq Composite up 1.3% to 26,690.62, and the Dow adding 0.28% to 54,036.93. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-aug-7-2026), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-august-7-nasdaq-dow-sp-500-july-jobs-report-surprises-100009572.html))
- Fed policy outlook: rate futures show September rate-hike odds fell to about 42-44% (from 55-57% the prior day and ~67% a week earlier); the probability the Fed holds steady rose to roughly 60%. Money markets still price in a possible hike later in 2026, but not before December. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/us-rate-futures-cut-chances-124656547.html))
- The Fed remains internally divided: the FOMC held rates at 3.50%-3.75% on a 9-3 vote in late July, and a minority of officials continue to push for a near-term hike as inflation stays elevated. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-07/fed-split-on-rate-hikes-deepens-as-high-inflation-tests-patience))
- Australia: the ASX 200 eased 0.09% to 9,263.60, with financials, healthcare and industrials leading losses; the RBA's next rate decision is due Tuesday, 11 August, with the big four banks expecting a hold after June-quarter CPI eased to 3.8% y/y. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/australia-stocks-lower-at-close-of-trade-spasx-200-down-009-4845143), [Aussie](https://www.aussie.com.au/insights/news/expert-predictions-rba-rates/))
- Commodities: WTI crude extended its rally toward ~US$78/bbl, building on a jump tied to a restrictive Iranian draft plan for the Strait of Hormuz; gold rose over 2.5% to roughly US$4,350/oz as the weak jobs data boosted safe-haven flows. ([Motley Fool Australia](https://www.fool.com.au/2026/08/07/5-things-to-watch-on-the-asx-200-on-friday-07-august-2026/))
- Sector/company news: ASX energy majors Santos (STO) and Woodside Energy (WDS) were positioned to benefit from the oil price jump, while Bell Potter reiterated a "hold" rating and 95-cent price target on Beach Energy (BPT) following a mixed FY2026 result. ([Motley Fool Australia](https://www.fool.com.au/2026/08/07/5-things-to-watch-on-the-asx-200-on-friday-07-august-2026/))

## Investment Implications
- The weaker-than-expected labour market reduces near-term Fed hike risk, which markets read as broadly supportive of the record-high US equity levels.
- Persistent internal Fed dissent and elevated inflation keep policy uncertainty elevated; a hike remains priced in by money markets for later in 2026, so rate-sensitive sectors could stay volatile.
- Rising oil prices linked to Strait of Hormuz tensions add a geopolitical risk premium to energy markets — a tailwind for energy equities but a potential headwind for broader inflation expectations.

## Watchlist for Today
- RBA's cash rate decision on Tuesday, 11 August (2:30pm AEST) — consensus expects a hold after June CPI eased to 3.8%.
- Further Fed commentary following the split 9-3 FOMC vote, for signals on the December meeting.
- Strait of Hormuz developments and their effect on oil prices and ASX energy stocks (Woodside, Santos, Beach Energy).
