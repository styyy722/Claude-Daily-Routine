# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-09-28

## Executive Summary
- US equities fell across the board Monday as Treasury yields raced to near-20-year highs and oil spiked after President Trump rebuffed Iran's Strait of Hormuz peace proposal, telling Axios Iran had "overplayed its hand": S&P 500 -0.77% to 7,683.69, Dow -0.67% (-347pts) to 51,481.51, Nasdaq Composite -0.92% to 26,820.38.
- The 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 5.21% (a level not seen since 2007) and the 30-year hit a 2004 high, as money markets raised bets on a further Fed rate hike in October on persistent inflation concerns; gold slumped to a seven-week low ($4,148.69/oz, -3.19%) as a result.
- The ASX 200 bucked the global trend, rising 0.17% to 8,679.7 on gains in healthcare, utilities and financials, as markets priced a ~90% probability of a 25bp RBA rate hike to 4.60% at Tuesday's (29 September) decision.

## Key Financial News
- **Trump rebuffs Iran's Hormuz proposal, oil jumps**: Trump told Axios on Sunday that Iran had "overplayed its hand" after Tehran pushed for talks limited to the Strait of Hormuz and the US naval blockade, while the administration is instead demanding nuclear concessions; Brent crude jumped back toward $107/bbl on the news, and WTI spiked as high as $96.54 intraday before settling at $92.60. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-28-2026))
- **Treasury yields surge to multi-decade highs**: The 10-year yield rose to 5.21%, a level not seen since 2007, while the 30-year touched a 2004 high, as traders increased bets on a further Fed rate hike as soon as October on persistent inflation pressure. ([Babypips](https://www.babypips.com/news/headline-us-treasury-yields-september-28-2026-selloff-iran-fed-hike))
- **Wall Street falls broadly**: The S&P 500 (-0.77%), Dow (-0.67%, -347pts) and Nasdaq Composite (-0.92%) all declined Monday, with commentary pointing to a mix of Fed rate-hike bets, the Iran-driven energy crisis, growing US budget deficits and the scale of AI-buildout capital spending as factors unsettling investors. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-september-28-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-080420627.html))
- **Gold slumps to seven-week low**: Gold fell 3.19% to $4,148.69/oz, its lowest since 5 August, as the oil-driven inflation scare reinforced higher-for-longer rate expectations and reduced the appeal of non-yielding bullion. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-monday-september-28-2026-gold-prices-slump-as-iran-tensions-and-oil-prices-rise-110730952.html))
- **ASX 200 bucks the global sell-off**: The index rose 0.17% to 8,679.7, led by healthcare (+1.5%), utilities (+1.2%) and financials (+1.2%), even as materials and technology lagged, with investors positioning ahead of Tuesday's RBA decision. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-28/asx-markets-business-live-news-september-28/107201818))
- **RBA hike odds firm at ~90% into Tuesday**: Money markets price roughly a 90% probability of a 25bp hike to 4.60% — which would be the RBA's highest cash rate since November 2011 — at the 29 September decision. ([IBTimes Australia](https://www.ibtimes.com.au/australian-shares-rba-rate-hike-460-percent-1875923))

## Investment Implications
- The combination of a near-20-year-high 10-year yield and a fresh oil spike is being read by markets as a genuine inflation-risk repricing rather than a growth scare, which argues for continued pressure on rate-sensitive and long-duration equity sectors until the Iran standoff or yields show signs of easing.
- Gold's slide to a seven-week low despite heightened Middle East geopolitical risk underscores that "higher-for-longer" rate expectations are currently dominating over traditional safe-haven demand.
- The ASX 200's resilience relative to Wall Street, led by defensives (healthcare, utilities) and financials, suggests local positioning is already largely built around an expected 25bp RBA hike; a surprise on Tuesday (no hike, or a larger move) would likely trigger an outsized reaction.
