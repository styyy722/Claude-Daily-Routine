## Key Financial News

- **Strait of Hormuz Closes Again:** Iran's IRGC fired on and set fire to the M/V GFS Galaxy, a Cyprus-flagged container ship transiting the Strait of Hormuz on Saturday, with one crew member reported missing. Iran declared the waterway closed until further notice. ([Times of Israel](https://www.timesofisrael.com/liveblog-july-11-2026/))
- **US Launches Third Strike of the Week:** US Central Command forces struck Iranian targets again around 7:15pm ET in response to the ship attack — the third round of US strikes on Iran this week, following the collapse of the Islamabad Memorandum ceasefire on 8 July. ([Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/world/2026/07/11/us-strikes-iran-it-declares-strait-hormuz-closed-fires-ship/))
- **Oman Pushes De-escalation Deal:** Ahead of the attack, Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi met Omani counterpart Sayyid Badr Albusaidi in Muscat to discuss a proposal for a two-corridor Hormuz traffic system — free navigation in a Southern Corridor, prior Iranian approval required in a Northern Corridor, with no tolls. The plan's status after Saturday's attack is unclear.
- **UN Warns of "Catastrophic" Escalation Risk:** UN Secretary-General António Guterres called the renewed hostilities "alarming," warning of consequences for regional and global security. The UN estimates roughly 6,000 seafarers remain stranded on vessels in the channel, with daily transits down sharply from the pre-crisis pace of ~130 ships/day. ([UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/07/1167898))
- **Friday's Session — Stocks Close Higher on AI Optimism:** The Dow (+149.60pts/+0.29% to 52,637.01), S&P 500 (+31.75pts/+0.42% to 7,575.39) and Nasdaq (+74.72pts/+0.29% to 26,281.61) all rose Friday as SK Hynix's US listing tested continued appetite for the AI-memory trade, even as investors weighed the (since-collapsed) US-Iran ceasefire talks. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/live/stock-market-today-friday-july-10-dow-sp-nasdaq-113921604.html))
- **Bull Market Intact Into the Weekend:** The S&P 500 finished the week +1.2% and sat roughly 0.45% below its all-time closing high, up 10.7% year-to-date; the "Mag 7" and broader tech led last week's rebound with a 4.9% gain, while small caps and materials lagged. ([Seeking Alpha](https://seekingalpha.com/article/4921380-the-1-minute-market-report-july-11-2026))
- **Yields Creeping Higher:** The US 10-year Treasury yield closed Friday near 4.56%; commentary flagged that a further move toward 5% would raise the odds of a hawkish Fed response and pressure richly-valued equities.
