# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-08-12

## Executive Summary
- US inflation cooled for a second straight month — July CPI rose 3.4% year-on-year (0.1% month-on-month), matching expectations — pushing traders to favour a Fed hold over a hike at the September meeting.
- Wall Street edged higher on the news: the S&P 500 gained 0.26% to a fresh closing high of 7,748.50 and the Nasdaq Composite added 0.54% to 26,588.49, while the Dow Jones slipped 0.04% to 53,770.27; AI-linked names led, with Super Micro Computer and CoreWeave surging around 19% and Lumentum jumping 14% on strong earnings.
- The ASX 200 fell 0.45% to 9,209.40 as A-REITs, consumer discretionary, and telco stocks led broad-based selling, even as the softer US CPI print offered a modestly supportive backdrop; Brent crude held near $90/bbl with the Strait of Hormuz still effectively closed to commercial shipping.

## Key Financial News

- **July CPI cools to 3.4% y/y:** US headline inflation eased for a second consecutive month to 3.4% annually (from 3.5% in June), with the monthly gain at just 0.1%; core CPI slowed to 2.5% y/y from 2.6%, matching consensus. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-wednesday-august-12-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-cpi-report-091555133.html))
- **Fed rate-hike odds fade:** Traders had been roughly split 50-50 on a September rate hike heading into the report; the softer print shifted sentiment toward the Fed holding steady, even though inflation remains above the 2% target. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-12/traders-brace-for-pivotal-cpi-as-fed-hike-hangs-in-balance))
- **Wall Street closes at fresh highs:** The S&P 500 rose 0.26% to a record 7,748.50 and the Nasdaq Composite climbed 0.54% to 26,588.49; the Dow was roughly flat, down 0.04% to 53,770.27. Industrials led sector gains while consumer cyclicals and communication services lagged. ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/08/12/stock-market-today-aug-12-stocks-edge-higher-as-inflation-data-eases-fed-rate-pressure/))
- **AI and tech earnings stand out:** Super Micro Computer and CoreWeave both surged about 19%, and Lumentum Holdings jumped 14% after results pointed to solid AI-related demand; Nvidia also rallied with the broader AI complex. Cisco Systems, Coherent, and Cerebras Systems also reported during the session. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-wednesday-august-12-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-cpi-report-091555133.html))
- **ASX 200 slips on broad-based selling:** Australia's benchmark fell 41.20 points (-0.45%) to 9,209.40, with decliners outnumbering advancers 604 to 492. A-REITs, consumer discretionary, and telco stocks led losses — Seek Ltd plunged 14.1% and Premier Investments fell 10.5% — while Codan (+8.8%) and Alcoa (+7.3%) bucked the trend. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/australia-stocks-lower-at-close-of-trade-spasx-200-down-045-4853720))
- **Strait of Hormuz disruption keeps oil firm:** US-Iran talks over reopening the Strait of Hormuz remained stalled, with the strait still effectively closed to commercial shipping under naval escort; Brent crude futures pushed toward $90/bbl on the continued supply disruption. ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/08/05/world/live-news/iran-war-trump))
- **Treasury yields little changed ahead of CPI:** The 10-year Treasury yield held steady around 4.69–4.70% as investors positioned for the inflation release, with only a marginal move once the data confirmed expectations. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/12/treasury-yields-inflation-cpi-data.html))

## Investment Implications

- A second straight month of cooling inflation strengthens the case for a September Fed hold, which has been supportive for both equities and gold near-term; a hotter print in coming months would quickly revive hike risk.
- The divergence between resilient US equities (fresh S&P 500 highs) and a softer ASX 200 highlights how the Hormuz-driven oil disruption and defensive/rate-sensitive sector weakness (A-REITs, telcos) are weighing more heavily on Australian markets than on US tech-led gains.
- Firm oil prices tied to the Strait of Hormuz closure remain a key upside inflation risk that could offset the disinflationary trend seen in the July CPI report if the disruption persists or escalates.
