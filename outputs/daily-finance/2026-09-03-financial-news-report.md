# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-09-03

## Executive Summary
- US equities extended their rebound (S&P 500 +1.06%, Dow +1.18%, Nasdaq +1.4%) as Treasury yields eased from multi-year highs, even as the US-Iran conflict around the Strait of Hormuz remained the dominant macro risk.
- Oil hit fresh six-week highs on Hormuz supply fears, while gold whipsawed — touching a near one-month low before recovering as the US dollar and yields pulled back.
- Fed rate-cut odds for September have collapsed to near zero, with markets now pricing roughly a 60% chance of a 25bp *hike* at the September 15–16 FOMC meeting on resilient labour data and energy-driven inflation pressure.

## Key Financial News
- **US equities rally further**: All three major US indices posted strong gains as Treasury yields pulled back from recent highs, extending Wednesday's gains and continuing to recover from last week's Iran-driven sell-off. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/stock-market-today-live-updates.html))
- **US-Iran conflict escalates then shows signs of easing**: The US struck roughly 100 IRGC-linked targets near the Strait of Hormuz on September 1, including Iranian government tankers for the first time; Iran retaliated with missiles and drones against US bases in Jordan, Bahrain and Kuwait. By Thursday, some signs of de-escalation helped lift ASX mining, gold and bank stocks. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-01/two-oil-supertankers-hit-by-projectiles-in-hormuz-marisks-says), [IG](https://www.ig.com/en-ch/news-and-trade-ideas/asx-200-afternoon-report--3-september-2026-260831))
- **ASX 200 rebounds**: Australian shares rose as easing Hormuz tensions lifted miners and gold names, with banks also rebounding after August's sharp sell-off. ([Market Index](https://www.marketindex.com.au/news/asx-200-live-today-thursday-3rd-september))
- **Fed policy path flips toward a hike**: Rate-cut odds for September have fallen to roughly 1%, with markets instead pricing about a 60% chance of a 25bp hike on September 15–16, driven by resilient employment data and energy-shock-driven inflation risk. VP JD Vance publicly called for the Fed to cut rates to ease housing affordability, adding political pressure in the opposite direction. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/economy/policy/articles/fed-rate-cut-odds-slashed-162150703.html))
- **Bond yields ease from highs**: The US 10-year yield eased to 4.76%, down from recent multi-year highs near 4.79-4.81%, giving equities room to rally.
- **Oil surges on supply risk**: WTI rose to roughly $93.05/bbl (+2.2%), a fresh six-week high, as the Hormuz conflict raised concerns about shipping and crude flows despite crude still reaching the market. ([EnergyNow](https://energynow.com/2026/09/oil-climbs-to-fresh-six-week-highs-as-u-s-iran-escalation-deepens-hormuz-supply-fears/))
- **Corporate earnings in focus**: Broadcom, Snowflake and HPE reported results; Broadcom shares fell despite beating earnings estimates, while AI-related names broadly supported the Nasdaq's advance. ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/09/03/stock-market-midday-sept-3-stocks-rally-as-treasury-yields-fall-broadcom-falls-despite-earnings-beat/))

## Investment Implications
- A hawkish repricing of Fed expectations (hike odds rising, cut odds near zero) could pressure rate-sensitive sectors even as headline indices rally on easing yields for now.
- Continued Strait of Hormuz volatility keeps energy and shipping costs as a live tail risk for inflation and corporate margins, despite the day's partial de-escalation.
- Gold's whipsaw between a one-month low and a rebound underscores how sensitive safe-haven demand remains to shifting Fed and geopolitical signals.
