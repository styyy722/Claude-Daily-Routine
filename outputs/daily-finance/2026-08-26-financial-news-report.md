## Key Financial News

- **PCE inflation stays sticky:** The headline PCE price index rose 0.2% month-over-month and 3.7% year-over-year in July, while core PCE (ex food and energy) held at 3.3% year-over-year, keeping inflation well above the Fed's 2% target and reinforcing a cautious tone ahead of Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's Jackson Hole address on Friday.
- **Markets tread water ahead of Nvidia:** The S&P 500 closed essentially flat at 7,675.70 and the Nasdaq Composite dipped 0.08% to 26,130.20, with Nvidia itself down about 1.28% intraday as investors awaited its after-market Q2 report, a key bellwether for the AI trade.
- **Australia's CPI surprises hawkish on the core measure:** Headline CPI eased to 3.5% year-on-year for July (from 3.8% in June), but trimmed-mean core inflation held steady at 3.6%, prompting economists at Goldman Sachs and Commonwealth Bank to abandon expectations of no further RBA moves this year and pencil in a possible hike as soon as September.
- **Oil extends slide:** Brent crude fell 2.6% to $86.20/barrel and WTI dropped 2.5% to $80.30/barrel, the third straight decline, as Iran-Oman talks raised hopes of an eventual Strait of Hormuz reopening.
- **Gold eases from highs:** Spot gold pulled back to around $4,614.31/oz after touching over $4,700 earlier in the session, still elevated but off its intraday peak.
- **ASX 200 reverses on rate-hike risk:** The index fell 0.4% to 9,128, its first drop in three sessions, as the CPI report's hot core reading revived RBA tightening expectations and weighed on rate-sensitive sectors.

## Investment Implications

- Sticky US PCE inflation keeps pressure on the Fed to maintain a cautious stance, with Chair Warsh's Friday Jackson Hole speech now a key focus for the rate-path outlook.
- Nvidia's after-hours earnings are a pivotal near-term catalyst; the muted pre-earnings trading suggests markets are positioning cautiously rather than pricing in a clear direction.
- Australia's hot core CPI print materially raises the risk of an earlier-than-expected RBA hike, a headwind for rate-sensitive ASX sectors and a potential support for the AUD in the near term.
