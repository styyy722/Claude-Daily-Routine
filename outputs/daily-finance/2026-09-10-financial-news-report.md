- **US stocks fall for a fourth straight day**: The S&P 500 lost 0.58% to 7,591.70, the Dow dropped 316.56 points (-0.60%) to 52,064.10, and the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.65% to 26,081.72, as oil surging past $100/bbl fanned inflation fears. ## Investment Implications
- The combination of $100+ oil and a Treasury yield nearing 5% is now squeezing equities on two fronts at once — inflation risk and higher discount rates — and has driven a fourth consecutive down day for US benchmarks.
- Markets are moving quickly to price in an RBA hike (~70% odds for September 29), which is compounding pressure on the ASX even as it also signals confidence the domestic economy can absorb higher rates.
- With ASX losses broad-based and US stocks falling in tandem with oil and yields, markets currently appear to be treating the Middle East conflict as a macro inflation shock rather than an isolated sector event — a dynamic that could persist as long as oil stays elevated.
