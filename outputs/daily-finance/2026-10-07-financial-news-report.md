- **Wall Street eases off record highs**: The S&P 500 (-0.22% to 7,801.77), Nasdaq Composite (-0.22% to 27,538.69) and Dow (-0.66% to 51,179.87) all retreated from Tuesday's record closes; available coverage did not surface a single dominant catalyst for the pullback, which followed a multi-session run to fresh highs.

## Investment Implications
- A modest, broad-based pullback after several sessions of record closes, without a clear negative catalyst, is more consistent with near-term consolidation than a trend reversal; investors should watch whether indices stabilize or extend the move in coming sessions.
- The FOMC's September minutes may offer fresh insight into how individual policymakers' rate-path projections diverged at the last Summary of Economic Projections meeting; markets are likely to parse the text closely for forward guidance on the 27-28 October meeting.
- Gold's sharp one-day pullback alongside steady Treasury yields suggests profit-taking or reduced near-term safe-haven demand rather than a shift in rate expectations; this bears monitoring given gold's elevated level this year.
