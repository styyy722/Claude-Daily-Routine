# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-09-08

## Executive Summary
- US equities fell broadly (Dow -1.18%, S&P -0.58%, Nasdaq -0.32%) as escalating US-Iran tensions pushed oil higher and clouded the inflation outlook ahead of key US data later this week.

## Key Financial News
- **US equities slide on Middle East escalation**: The S&P 500 fell 0.58% to 7,673.52, the Dow dropped 1.18% (-628.18 points) to 52,786.07 and the Nasdaq lost 0.32% to 26,421.41, as Iran threatened retaliation against the US over ballistic missile capabilities and continued military activity in the Strait of Hormuz kept energy markets on edge. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-8-133744027.html))
- **Inflation outlook "murkier" on oil rally**: Capital.com senior analyst Kyle Rodda noted the inflation picture is becoming harder to read because of the rally in oil prices following the latest exchange of strikes between the US and Iran, complicating the Fed's rate path just ahead of key inflation data due later this week. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-8-133744027.html))
- **Canada imposes retaliatory tariffs**: Canada implemented retaliatory tariffs on US goods after trade negotiations collapsed, adding a fresh layer of macro uncertainty on top of the geopolitical risk already weighing on sentiment. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-8-133744027.html))
- **ASX 200 sinks to six-week low**: The index fell 1% to 8,920, with technology (-2%), financials (-1.9%) and REITs (-1.7%) the biggest drags, partially offset by defensive gains in utilities (+0.5%) and energy (+0.4%); PDI Gold (+3.8%) and Downer EDI (+3.1%) led gainers, while 4DMedical (-4.8%) and Judo Capital (-4.7%) lagged. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-08/asx-markets-business-live-news-september-8-2026/107127114))
- **Westpac joins RBA hike consensus**: Westpac became the final major Australian bank to forecast an RBA rate increase, predicting a 25-basis-point hike to 4.6% at the November meeting; chief economist Luci Ellis cited growing evidence of a more resilient household sector and data-centre investment as drivers of the shift. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-08/asx-markets-business-live-news-september-8-2026/107127114))
- **Australian sentiment and business conditions weaken**: Consumer sentiment fell sharply as fuel prices exceeded $2/litre for the first time since April, while business conditions turned negative for the first time in six years, adding to the case for caution even as the RBA leans toward tightening. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-08/asx-markets-business-live-news-september-8-2026/107127114))
- **Oil and yields extend recent moves**: WTI rose about 0.97% to roughly $92.37/bbl and Brent gained 1.4% to $98.34/bbl on Strait of Hormuz supply risk, while the US 10-year Treasury yield held near a two-decade high around 4.79% amid rising September Fed rate-hike expectations following last week's strong jobs report; gold slipped roughly 0.6% toward $4,420/oz as those same rate-hike expectations outweighed safe-haven demand. ([TradingEconomics – Oil](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/crude-oil), [Yahoo Finance – Gold](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-tuesday-september-8-2026-gold-moves-lower-following-fresh-escalations-with-iran-111035525.html))

## Investment Implications
- The simultaneous rise in oil prices and Treasury yields is squeezing risk assets from two directions: higher energy costs threaten to keep inflation elevated just as the Fed appears increasingly likely to hike rather than cut in September.
- The ASX's rotation out of rate-sensitive sectors (tech, financials, REITs) and into defensives (utilities, energy) reflects markets pricing in both a more hawkish RBA and ongoing global geopolitical risk simultaneously.
- Gold's pullback despite escalating Middle East tensions underscores that rate-hike expectations are currently the dominant driver for the metal, overriding its usual safe-haven appeal.
