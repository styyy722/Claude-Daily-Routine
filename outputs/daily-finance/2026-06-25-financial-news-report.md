# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-06-25

## Executive Summary
- Memory-chip shortage dominated markets: Micron's blowout Q3 earnings (revenue up roughly 4x y/y, gross margin above 80%) sent its shares up ~16-18%, while Apple fell ~6% and Microsoft ~3% after both hiked device prices (MacBook/iPad up $100–$300+; Xbox consoles) to offset soaring chip costs.
- US equities were mixed and roughly flat: S&P 500 essentially unchanged, Nasdaq fell as Mag7 weakness offset chip-sector strength, while the Dow posted a modest gain.
- Gold held above $4,000/oz and WTI crude slipped below $70/bbl for a fourth straight session, as a weaker US dollar and lower Treasury yields supported bullion even as oil demand concerns weighed on crude.

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/24/micron-mu-earnings-report-q3-2026.html), [Benzinga](https://www.benzinga.com/markets/equities/26/06/60108692/micron-rallies-apple-sinks-stock-market-thursday))

## Investment Implications
- The memory-chip shortage is becoming a tangible cost pass-through risk for consumer hardware makers, which could pressure margins or demand for Apple- and Microsoft-branded devices in coming quarters.
- Chip/memory suppliers (e.g., Micron) may continue to benefit from pricing power as "tight conditions" are expected to persist beyond 2027, according to Micron management commentary.
- Continued softness in Mag7 names alongside resilient broader indices suggests market leadership may be rotating toward semiconductor/AI infrastructure plays and away from device makers facing input-cost pressure.
