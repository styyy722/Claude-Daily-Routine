## Key Financial News

- **US-Iran tensions escalate:** A memorandum of understanding between the US and Iran expired Monday; President Trump said he didn't "see the war ending anytime soon" and warned of possible military action against Oman if it interferes with the Strait of Hormuz, sending Brent crude above $89–91/barrel and WTI up over $2 to around $84.50.
- **Long-dated yields surge:** The 30-year Treasury yield rose to 5.31%, its highest since June 2007, while the 10-year yield closed near 4.72%, as investors grew more concerned about mounting US federal debt, now approaching $40 trillion.
- **Equities slide for a third day:** The S&P 500 fell 0.52% to 7,745.06, the Nasdaq Composite dropped 0.32% to 26,644.91, and the Dow lost about 0.51% to 53,459.78, as rising yields and oil prices weighed on risk sentiment.
- **AI infrastructure deal:** Nvidia, OpenAI and SB Energy announced a roughly $105 billion data center deal in Ohio, underscoring continued heavy investment in AI infrastructure even as broader markets wobbled.
- **Notable movers:** Micron shares crossed $1,000 for the first time since early July; Anthropic reportedly said its Q2 revenue surged roughly 14-fold to $11.5 billion.
- **ASX 200 slips on earnings and rate jitters:** The index fell 0.46% to 9,073.2; National Australia Bank posted higher profit but flagged declining home lending, and JB Hi-Fi shares tumbled, dragging on sentiment as investors braced for a busy reporting week.
- **Gold and iron ore steady:** Gold ticked up modestly to around $4,440/oz following the softer US retail sales report last week, while iron ore held roughly flat near $95.34/tonne.

## Investment Implications

- Renewed US-Iran tensions add a fresh geopolitical risk premium to oil prices, which could feed through to inflation expectations and further pressure long-dated bond yields if the standoff escalates.
- The 30-year Treasury yield's climb to an 18-year high highlights growing investor unease about US fiscal sustainability, a dynamic that could keep borrowing costs elevated across markets regardless of near-term Fed policy moves.
- Continued mega-scale AI infrastructure investment (Nvidia/OpenAI/SB Energy) suggests capital spending on AI remains a key offsetting force for equities even as macro and geopolitical headwinds build.
