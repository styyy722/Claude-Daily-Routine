## Key Financial News

- **US-Canada trade tensions escalate:** Canada announced retaliatory tariffs on roughly $20 billion of US goods (15–50% on steel, dairy and electronics), prompting President Trump to call Canada "easily the most difficult and unreasonable" trading partner; crude oil was notably excluded from the retaliation list.
- **Moderna surges on cancer vaccine breakthrough:** Shares more than doubled after Moderna and Merck reported their personalized mRNA melanoma vaccine (intismeran), combined with Keytruda, prevented recurrence or spread of melanoma in high-risk patients — described as a "landmark moment" for cancer immunotherapy.
- **DICK'S Sporting Goods tumbles:** Shares fell about 30%, their worst day since 2023, after Q2 adjusted EPS of $3.53 and revenue of $5.59 billion missed estimates; the retailer cut its full-year sales and profit outlook, citing heavier promotions and fewer new product launches in athletic footwear.
- **Nvidia rallies ahead of earnings:** Nvidia shares snapped a seven-day losing streak, rising about 2% ahead of Wednesday's highly anticipated Q2 report, a key bellwether for AI-related equities.
- **Consumer and housing data soften:** US consumer confidence fell to 89.4, its lowest since January, while new home sales dropped to similar lows as mortgage rates stayed elevated — reinforcing expectations for a cautious tone from the Fed.
- **Bonds, gold and crypto:** Treasury yields eased to around 4.66% and oil prices fell sharply, supporting equities; gold held firm near $4,640/oz on dollar-debasement and Treasury-intervention concerns, while Bitcoin briefly topped $80,000 for the first time in three months.
- **ASX 200 gains on healthcare, IT:** Australian shares rose 0.68% to 9,164.6, with health care, IT and consumer staples leading; ARB Corporation was the standout performer, up 12.44%.

## Investment Implications

- Falling Treasury yields and oil prices are providing a cushion for equities even as US-Canada trade tensions add headline risk to tariff-sensitive sectors.
- Nvidia's Wednesday earnings remain a key near-term catalyst for the broader AI trade, which has driven much of this year's tech-sector gains.
- Softening US consumer confidence and housing data increase the focus on Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's first Jackson Hole address on Friday for signals on the rate path.
