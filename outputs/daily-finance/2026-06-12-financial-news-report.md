# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-06-12

## Executive Summary
- US stocks rose for a second straight day (Dow +0.7%, S&P 500 +0.5%, Nasdaq +0.31%) as SpaceX's blockbuster Nasdaq debut and growing optimism over a US-Iran peace deal lifted sentiment.
- Oil prices fell sharply (WTI down towards ~$87-90/bbl, -3% to -5%) after Pakistan's PM said a final US-Iran deal text had been reached, with Trump expecting to sign a settlement over the weekend that could reopen the Strait of Hormuz.
- The ASX 200 surged 1.98% to a record-area close of 8,804.0, riding Thursday's huge Wall Street rally plus a rebound in gold miners as bullion bounced back above $4,200/oz on the oil-driven de-escalation trade.

## Key Financial News

- **SpaceX's Historic Nasdaq Debut:** SpaceX (ticker SPCX) priced its IPO at $135/share, raising roughly $75 billion in the largest IPO in Wall Street history. Shares opened at $150, surged more than 20% intraday, and closed at $161.11, up about 19% on the day, implying a market valuation in the region of $1.7-2.1 trillion. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/12/spacex-ipo-spcx-live-updates.html), [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-june-12-2026))
- **Wall Street Extends Gains:** The Dow rose 353.51 points (+0.70%) to 51,202.26, the S&P 500 added 0.50% to 7,431.46, and the Nasdaq Composite gained 0.31% to 25,888.84, building on Thursday's sharp rally as the SpaceX listing and Iran de-escalation hopes offset lingering inflation concerns. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-june-12-2026))
- **US-Iran Peace Deal Nears:** Pakistan's prime minister said the final text of a US-Iran peace deal had been agreed, with President Trump expecting to sign it within days, potentially this weekend in Europe. Trump also said he had called off planned strikes on Iran's Kharg Island oil hub after a "great settlement" was reached, raising hopes the Strait of Hormuz will stay open. ([RFE/RL](https://www.rferl.org/a/iran-war-us-hormuz-oil-blockade-gulf-israel/33640284.html), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/06/10/trump-iran-deal-oil-markets-stocks.html))
- **Oil Slides on De-escalation:** WTI and Brent crude extended Thursday's declines, with WTI falling toward the high-$80s (from ~$91 earlier in the week) and Brent dropping below $90/bbl, as easing Strait of Hormuz disruption risk pulled the geopolitical premium out of oil. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/price-of-oil-06-12-2026/), [Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/crude-oil/news/539423))
- **Gold Rebounds:** Bullion bounced back above $4,200/oz (roughly +2.5% on the session) after Wednesday-Thursday's sharp slide, as the unwind of the oil-driven risk-off trade and a steadier US dollar drew renewed haven demand. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/current-price-of-gold-06-12-2026/), [The Motley Fool AU](https://www.fool.com.au/2026/06/12/5-things-to-watch-on-the-asx-200-on-friday-12-june-2026/))
- **ASX 200 Jumps Nearly 2%:** The benchmark surged 170.80 points (+1.98%) to 8,804.00, its strongest session in months, tracking Thursday's ~900-point Dow surge. Gold miners Evolution Mining and Newmont led gains as bullion rebounded, while energy names Santos and Woodside lagged on the slump in oil prices. ([The Motley Fool AU](https://www.fool.com.au/2026/06/12/5-things-to-watch-on-the-asx-200-on-friday-12-june-2026/))
- **Bond Yields Ease, AUD/USD Steady:** The US 10-year Treasury yield eased to around 4.47%, down roughly 10 basis points from the prior session, as easing oil-driven inflation risk reduced near-term rate-hike pressure ahead of next week's FOMC meeting. AUD/USD was little changed at ~0.7049 (+0.01%). ([Trading Economics – Yields](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/government-bond-yield), [exchange-rates.org](https://www.exchange-rates.org/exchange-rate-history/aud-usd-2026))

## Investment Implications

- **Risk-On Sentiment Has Returned, but the Trigger Is Still a Headline:** With both the equity rally and the oil sell-off hinging on a deal that has not yet been formally signed, markets remain highly sensitive to any setback in US-Iran negotiations over the coming days.
- **A De-escalation in Oil Could Ease the Inflation Narrative:** If WTI/Brent hold in the high-$80s rather than retesting $95+, the energy-driven inflation shock seen earlier in the week may prove temporary — a favourable backdrop for incoming Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's first FOMC meeting on 16-17 June.
- **Mega-IPO Supply Is a New Market Dynamic:** SpaceX's record listing (following other large 2026 tech IPOs) adds a fresh source of index-level volatility and could absorb liquidity that might otherwise flow into existing large-cap tech names.

## Watchlist for Monday, 15 June 2026

- **Formal Signing of the US-Iran Deal:** Confirmation (or collapse) of the reported peace agreement over the weekend will likely drive the next major moves in oil, gold and global equities.
- **FOMC Meeting (16-17 June):** The first meeting under new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh is a key risk event — watch Treasury yields and rate-hike pricing for signs of how the Fed weighs the recent inflation data against easing oil prices.
- **SpaceX (SPCX) Follow-Through:** Watch whether SpaceX's shares hold their post-IPO pop, as the stock's performance may set the tone for sentiment around other anticipated 2026 mega-IPOs.
