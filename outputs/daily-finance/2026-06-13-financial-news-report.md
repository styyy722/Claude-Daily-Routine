# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-06-13

## Executive Summary
- Saturday was a non-trading day for US and Australian equity markets; Friday's rally stands as the last close (Dow +0.70%, S&P 500 +0.50%, ASX 200 +1.98% to a record 8,804.0).
- The US-Iran peace deal timeline became contested over the weekend: President Trump said an agreement would be signed Sunday and the Strait of Hormuz would reopen immediately after, but Iran's Foreign Ministry said no signing was scheduled for Sunday.
- Israel carried out fresh strikes across southern Lebanon, killing at least five people despite the US-brokered ceasefire, a reminder that broader Middle East risk remains unresolved even as the Iran talks progress.

## Key Financial News

- **Wall Street and ASX 200 Close the Week Strong:** Friday's session was the last trading day before the weekend — the Dow rose 353.51 points (+0.70%) to 51,202.26, the S&P 500 gained 0.50% to 7,431.46, and the Nasdaq Composite added 0.31% to 25,888.84, while the ASX 200 jumped 1.98% (+170.80 pts) to a record-area 8,804.00. Both equity markets remained closed on Saturday. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-june-12-2026))
- **US-Iran Deal Signing Timeline Disputed:** President Trump said the agreement to end the US-Israel-Iran conflict was "scheduled to get signed tomorrow" (Sunday, 14 June), with the Strait of Hormuz to be "open to all" immediately afterward. However, Iran's Foreign Ministry spokesperson said no signing was planned for Sunday and that negotiators were not yet travelling to Geneva, contradicting the US timeline. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/13/trump-says-iran-deal-to-be-signed-tomorrow-contradicting-iranian-official))
- **Iran Says Deal "Never Been Closer":** Iranian Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said a memorandum of understanding — reportedly including the unfreezing of Iranian assets — has never been closer to completion. A Trump administration official separately put the odds of a signing at roughly 80%, with terms said to cover reopening Hormuz, lifting the naval blockade, and dismantling Iran's nuclear program. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/liveblog/2026/6/13/iran-war-live-us-tehran-signal-peace-deal-within-reach-but-not-signed-yet))
- **Israel Strikes Southern Lebanon Despite Ceasefire:** Israeli forces struck multiple locations across southern Lebanon on Saturday, killing at least five people — including the mayor of Ar-Rihan — and issued evacuation orders for more than 20 areas, including Nabatieh, ahead of further raids. Further Israel-Lebanon talks are scheduled later this month. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/6/13/one-killed-as-israel-hits-south-lebanon-issues-forced-displacement-orders))
- **Oil at an Eight-Week Low Heading Into the Weekend:** WTI crude fell more than 4% to below $84/bbl on Friday (Brent near $88/bbl), its lowest level in roughly eight weeks, as growing confidence in a US-Iran deal eased fears of a prolonged Strait of Hormuz disruption. No futures trading occurred over the weekend. ([Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/crude-oil))
- **Gold Holds Near $4,220/oz:** Bullion was little changed over the weekend at roughly $4,218-4,224/oz, broadly consolidating Friday's rebound as the unwind of the oil-driven risk-off trade continued. ([BusinessToday](https://www.businesstoday.in/personal-finance/investment/story/gold-silver-rates-today-june-13-2026-check-latest-prices-for-24k-22k-18k-in-delhi-mumbai-chennai-kolkata-536697-2026-06-13))
- **AUD/USD Steady Near 0.7044:** The Australian dollar was little changed over the weekend (-0.07% vs Friday's ~0.7049), with FX markets quiet ahead of the resumption of full trading and the RBA's 15-16 June policy meeting. ([exchange-rates.org](https://www.exchange-rates.org/exchange-rate-history/aud-usd-2026))

## Investment Implications

- **Headline Risk Dominates Into the New Week:** With Washington and Tehran offering conflicting signing timelines, oil, gold and equities could see sharp moves on Monday depending on whether Sunday's signing proceeds, slips, or collapses entirely.
- **Energy De-escalation Trade Has Further Room if Confirmed:** A formal, durable reopening of the Strait of Hormuz would likely extend oil's slide toward multi-month lows and further ease the inflation narrative that has weighed on rate-cut expectations — a tailwind for risk assets.
- **Middle East Risk Premium Remains Asymmetric:** Continued Israeli strikes in Lebanon, even alongside progress on the separate Iran deal, are a reminder that broader regional tensions could quickly re-price energy and safe-haven assets if the situation escalates.

## Watchlist for Sunday-Monday, 14-15 June 2026

- **US-Iran MOU Signing:** Watch for confirmation (or denial) of the reported Sunday signing and any detail on Strait of Hormuz access, asset unfreezing and sanctions relief — the key swing factor for Monday's market open.
- **Lebanon Ceasefire Stability:** Further Israeli strikes or a Hezbollah response could complicate the broader Iran narrative and push oil and gold higher if the conflict widens.
- **RBA Policy Meeting (15-16 June):** The Reserve Bank's policy decision, due 16 June at 2:30pm AEST, will be in focus for AUD/USD and ASX-listed financials as the meeting gets underway Monday.
