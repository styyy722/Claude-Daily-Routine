# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-07-21

## Executive Summary
- US equities rallied on a semiconductor-led rebound, with the Nasdaq up 1.29%, S&P 500 up 0.89%, and Dow up 0.74%, as investors positioned ahead of major tech earnings (Alphabet due Wednesday) and Nvidia gained on news of a stake in neocloud provider Nebius.
- Gold rebounded above $4,050/oz and oil (WTI ~$84.29/bbl, Brent ~$90-91/bbl) pushed higher amid renewed US-Iran tensions, while the 10-year Treasury yield pushed above 4.6% as markets raised the odds of a September Fed rate hike to roughly 55%.
- The ASX 200 was essentially flat (+0.02%) at 8,793.3, with gains in gold, IT, and metals & mining offsetting weakness in healthcare and banks; President Trump also announced 50% tariffs on a range of Canadian goods (excluding oil), effective in 30 days.

## Key Financial News

- **Chip-Led Rally:** US markets rose broadly as semiconductor stocks extended their rebound; Nvidia climbed nearly 2% after disclosing a stake in neocloud provider Nebius, lifting the Nasdaq to its best session in weeks. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-july-21-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-084631499.html))
- **Tech Earnings Ahead:** Investors positioned ahead of a heavy week of Big Tech earnings, with Alphabet's report due Wednesday drawing particular attention to AI capex guidance. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-july-21-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-084631499.html))
- **Trump's Canada Tariffs:** The administration announced 50% tariffs on a range of Canadian goods, including beer, hockey sticks, milk, and chemicals, taking effect in 30 days; Canadian oil imports were exempted. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-july-21-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-084631499.html))
- **Fed Rate Path:** CME FedWatch data showed the market still expects the Fed to hold rates at next week's meeting, though the probability of a 25bp hike was pegged at around 16.6%; separately, other market commentary noted the odds of a September hike had risen to roughly 55%, up from 51% a day earlier. ([Yahoo Finance – Gold](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-tuesday-july-21-2026-gold-hovers-above-4000-ahead-of-next-weeks-fed-meeting-153613363.html))
- **Treasury Yields Climb:** The US 10-year Treasury yield pushed above 4.6% intraday as markets repriced the odds of continued Fed tightening amid firmer energy prices. ([CRFB](https://www.crfb.org/blogs/10-year-treasury-yield-eclipsed-46))
- **Gold Steadies Near $4,000:** Spot gold rebounded to around $4,050-4,063/oz on Tuesday after touching $4,013 at the open, as investors weighed potential US-Iran peace talks against ongoing geopolitical risk ahead of next week's Fed decision. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/select/the-price-of-gold-today-july-21-2026/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-tuesday-july-21-2026-gold-hovers-above-4000-ahead-of-next-weeks-fed-meeting-153613363.html))
- **Oil Firms on Iran Tensions:** WTI rose about 2.2% to roughly $84.29/bbl and Brent traded near $90-91/bbl, with prices supported by the latest flare-up in the US-Iran conflict even as some reports noted prices easing slightly intraday. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/price-of-oil-07-21-2026/); [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-july-21-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-084631499.html))
