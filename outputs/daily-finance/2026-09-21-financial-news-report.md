# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-09-21

## Executive Summary
- US equities rallied hard as tech and communication services led gains ahead of the Trump-Xi summit: S&P 500 +1.49% to 7,764.70, Nasdaq Composite +2.26% to a record close of 27,122.09, Dow +0.71%/+366.19pts to 52,048.83, while the 10-year Treasury yield eased over 4bp to ~4.95% from last week's post-2007 high.

## Key Financial News
- **US equities surge on tech/comm-services strength ahead of Trump-Xi summit**: The S&P 500 climbed 1.49% to 7,764.70, the Nasdaq Composite jumped 2.26% to a record close of 27,122.09 (its first record since June), and the Dow Jones added 366.19pts (+0.71%) to 52,048.83, as Treasury Secretary Bessent said talks with China had been "successful" ahead of the expected Trump-Xi meeting in Washington later this week. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/20/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [US News](https://www.usnews.com/news/business/articles/2026-09-21/stocks-advance-after-bessent-says-talks-with-china-were-successful-before-trump-xi-meeting))
- **Oil slides sharply on Strait of Hormuz reopening and Iran diplomacy signal**: WTI had its worst day in more than a month, falling as much as 5%+, with sources ranging from roughly $93.86 to $101.85/bbl and Brent from roughly $100.46 to $104.40/bbl, after resumed traffic through the Strait of Hormuz, profit-taking following the recent Middle East-driven rally, and Trump signalling openness to meeting Iranian President Pezeshkian. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-21-135426827.html), [Saxo Bank](https://www.home.saxo/content/articles/macro/market-quick-take---oil-extends-its-slide-before-the-trump-xi-summit---21-september-2026-21092026))
- **10-year Treasury yield eases from post-2007 high**: The benchmark yield fell more than 4bp to about 4.951%, retreating further from last week's 19-year high of 5.041% hit after the Fed's rate hike. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/21/treasury-yields-government-bonds.html))
- **ASX 200 closes flat as banks rally on RBA rate-hike bets**: The ASX 200 ended essentially unchanged at 8,731 (-0.01%), with Financials leading (ANZ +0.9%) after all four major banks (CBA, ANZ, NAB, Westpac) aligned on forecasts for a 25bp RBA hike to 4.6% on September 29; ANZ additionally expects a follow-up hike in November taking the cash rate to 4.85%. Miners lagged (Rio Tinto -0.8%, Newmont -2%) despite firmer commodity prices. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-21/asx-markets-business-live-news/107174464))
- **Healthcare mixed, Perpetual plunges on rejected takeover bid**: Cochlear jumped 5.3% (ex-dividend) and CSL rose 1.5% on the ASX, while Telix Pharmaceuticals slid 12% after unveiling a $2.3 billion German isotope acquisition; wealth manager Perpetual plunged 15.1% after rejecting an improved takeover bid from EQT. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-21/asx-markets-business-live-news/107174464))
- **Gold slips as Fed/ECB tightening weighs**: Spot gold eased roughly 0.3-0.6% to a range of about $4,352-4,413/oz, pressured by recent rate hikes from the Fed and ECB even as lingering Middle East tension offered some support. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-monday-september-21-2026-gold-prices-slip-on-middle-east-tensions-133531697.html))
- **AUD/USD firms modestly, iron ore ticks higher**: The Australian dollar rose 0.1% to about 0.7122 as rate-hike expectations firmed, while benchmark iron ore added 0.8% to roughly $97.10/tonne. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-21/asx-markets-business-live-news/107174464))

## Investment Implications
- The Nasdaq's record close and broad tech/comm-services leadership suggest risk appetite remains strong heading into the Trump-Xi summit, but the outsized oil-price swing highlights how sensitive markets remain to geopolitical headlines out of the Middle East and US-China trade diplomacy.
- Easing Treasury yields alongside rallying equities point to a more supportive near-term backdrop for growth stocks, though the 10-year remains close to multi-decade highs and could reverse quickly if inflation data surprises.
- With all four major Australian banks now aligned on a September 29 RBA hike (and ANZ flagging a follow-up in November), AUD and rate-sensitive ASX sectors (banks, REITs) are likely to stay in focus over coming sessions.
