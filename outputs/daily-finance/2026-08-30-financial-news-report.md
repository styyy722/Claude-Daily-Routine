## Key Financial News

- **Rate-hike odds keep climbing over the weekend:** September Fed rate-hike odds firmed to roughly 55.7% per CME FedWatch (from ~35.4% before Friday's Jackson Hole speech), with Kalshi prediction-market pricing around 48%, as traders continued to digest Warsh's hawkish remarks into the weekend. ([SmallWorldFS](https://www.smallworldfs.com/blog/2026/08/30/fed-rate-hike-odds-jump-to-55-7-after-warsh-s-jackson-hole-speech/))
- **Warsh's inflation warning still the dominant driver:** Fed Chair Kevin Warsh reiterated that inflation remains "too high" and signalled the Fed may need to raise rates further, framing price stability as the central bank's predominant focus — a message markets are still repricing two days later. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/kevin-warsh-jackson-hole-fed-inflation-rate-hike.html))
- **Treasury yields hold near multi-week highs:** The 2-year yield remains near its highest level since July at around 4.30-4.35%, while the 10-year sits in the 4.67-4.72% range; no new weekend trading, but positioning continues to reflect the hawkish repricing.
- **China PMI is the next major data point:** China's NBS Manufacturing PMI for August, due Monday 9:30am China time (Sunday 9:30pm ET), will be closely watched after July's 49.2 print signalled continued contraction; a weak read would add to a mixed global growth picture ahead of the Fed's September meeting. ([Finance Calendar](https://www.financecalendar.com/event/china-official-pmi-august-2026/))
- **Oil little changed into the weekend:** WTI crude held around $83.44/bbl (Friday's close), broadly flat, with markets weighing easing near-term Middle East supply fears against periodic headline risk from the Russia-Ukraine conflict.
- **Gold still nursing its post-Warsh pullback:** Bullion remains near $4,454.94/oz, roughly 3% below its pre-speech level, after the dollar's strengthening on hawkish Fed repricing took some shine off the metal's earlier debasement/buyback-driven rally.
- **Week ahead brings a heavy US data calendar:** Beyond China's PMIs, the week of 31 August–4 September brings US ISM manufacturing, JOLTS, ADP and the August jobs report, a data sequence markets will use to confirm or challenge the post-Warsh rate-hike repricing. ([Newsquawk](https://www.newsquawk.com/headlines/newsquawk-weekly-economic-calendar---31st-august---4th-september-2026))

## Investment Implications

- With September rate-hike odds now above 55%, rate-sensitive assets (growth/tech equities, gold, long-duration bonds) are likely to stay volatile into the 15-16 September FOMC meeting; confirmation or reversal will hinge on the data releases in the coming week rather than further Fed commentary.
- A weak China PMI print Monday would compound growth concerns already weighing on iron ore and could pressure AUD and Australian resources names at the open, even as local rate-hike bets (ahead of the RBA's 28-29 September meeting) support the currency from the other direction.
- Gold's pullback from three-month highs may extend if Monday's data (China PMI, and later in the week US ISM/jobs) reinforces dollar strength; a soft US data run, conversely, could quickly revive the metal's rally.
