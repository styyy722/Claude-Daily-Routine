# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-08-27

## Executive Summary
- Nvidia's blockbuster earnings (guiding to ~70% FY2028 revenue growth) ignited a tech rally, sending the Nasdaq up 1.57% and S&P 500 up 0.72% to a fresh high; Salesforce and CrowdStrike surged on strong software results.
- A Cleveland Fed official renewed calls for higher rates after inflation data showed prices running near 3% annualized, tempering the AI-driven optimism.
- The ASX 200 fell 0.98% to 9,038.2 as hotter-than-expected July CPI data raised the odds of an RBA rate hike as soon as next month.

## Key Financial News

- **Nvidia beats and guides higher:** Shares jumped roughly 8–9% after Q2 results beat expectations and management guided to about $108 billion of Q3 revenue, with FY2028 revenue growth projected near 70% versus the ~44% analysts had expected — easing AI-bubble concerns and lifting chip peers Broadcom (+4.5%), SK Hynix (+2%) and Intel (+4%).
- **Software stocks roar back:** Salesforce jumped about 22% after beating estimates and raising full-year guidance to $46.1–$46.4 billion, with its CEO dismissing "SaaSpocalypse" fears; CrowdStrike rose about 20% on record net-new annual recurring revenue of $333 million and raised full-year guidance to $6.60–$6.61 billion; Okta surged 28%, leading the sector.
- **Fed officials still flag inflation risk:** Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack repeated her call for higher interest rates, saying recent data showing inflation running near 3% annualized means the Fed remains too far from its goal and should tighten policy further.
- **Treasury yields and commodities:** The US 10-year yield edged up to around 4.65%; gold eased about 0.5% to roughly $4,638.60/oz and WTI crude slipped about 0.5% to near $83.14/bbl.
- **Australia's CPI surprises to the upside:** July monthly CPI rose 1.0% versus a forecast 0.8%, with annual inflation easing to 3.5% but still above the 3.3% expected; trimmed-mean (core) inflation rose 0.5% in the month, lifting annual underlying inflation to 3.6% and raising market-implied odds of an RBA hike next month to about 27% (from 17%).
- **ASX 200 slides on rate-hike fears:** The index fell 0.98% to 9,038.2, with consumer discretionary (-3.18%), technology (-2.38%) and gold stocks (-1.54%) leading declines; only healthcare (+0.23%) and industrials (+0.03%) held up. Ramsay Health Care (+13.72%), Domino's Pizza Enterprises (+7.98%) and Tabcorp (+5.49%) were standout gainers.
- **Corporate news abroad:** French drinks group Pernod Ricard reported a 3.9% year-on-year decline in full-year net sales, citing weak demand in the US and China; Wendy's shares fell over 14% after reports that Trian Fund Management abandoned its takeover plans.

## Investment Implications

- Nvidia's guidance materially reduces near-term AI-bubble concerns and could extend the tech-led rally, but hawkish Fed commentary keeps rate-cut expectations in check and vulnerable to repricing.
- Australia's hotter core CPI print is a meaningful hawkish surprise for the RBA, raising the risk of tighter-than-expected Australian monetary policy and near-term pressure on rate-sensitive ASX sectors.
- Divergent central bank signals — a US Fed still wary of inflation and an RBA facing upside inflation surprises — could add volatility to AUD/USD and cross-asset positioning in the near term.
