## Key Financial News
- The ASX 200 closed down 1.79% at 8,883.19, its sharpest daily drop since the start of October, as materials led the slide (-3.86%): BHP fell 4.95%, Fortescue 5.55% and Rio Tinto 2.06%, with sources citing multi-month lows in iron ore and copper prices.
- Australian banks were broadly weaker — sources differ on magnitude, with one citing the ASX 200 Banks sub-index down 1.12% and another showing NAB -2.8%, Westpac -1.4%, ANZ -0.8% and CBA -0.4% — as hawkish RBA minutes reinforced expectations that the cash rate (hiked to 4.85% on November 3) could stay higher for longer.
- US stocks extended losses into Tuesday's close, with the Dow down as much as ~1.9% intraday and the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite both down over 1.3%, as investors de-risked ahead of Nvidia's after-the-bell fiscal Q3 FY2027 report — its first major event since CEO Jensen Huang's resignation.
- Nvidia shares fell more than 3% early before paring losses to roughly -2.5% after Huang said he would return as CEO if the board decided it was in shareholders' best interests; interim CEO Colette Kress is set to host the earnings call, with analysts expecting about $2.47 EPS on roughly $109.15 billion in revenue against prior guidance of $108.0 billion (±2%), and options pricing an ~8.5% share-price swing on the results.
- US October advance retail sales rose 0.2% month-over-month (slightly above the 0.1% expected), or 0.5% excluding autos and gas, while September's reading was revised up to 0.8%, pointing to still-resilient consumer spending.
- Software stocks underperformed, with the IGV software ETF down nearly 3% after OpenAI released new GPT models, pressuring Salesforce, ServiceNow and Intuit; separately, St. Louis Fed President Alberto Musalem called for interest rate hikes sooner rather than later, an unusually hawkish signal.
- Away from the AI/tech story, Disney and Home Depot both beat earnings estimates; Home Depot named CFO Richard McPhail as its next CEO, effective November 23. Anthropic's IPO remains on track to begin trading under ticker "ANT" on Thursday, November 19.

## Investment Implications
- Nvidia's after-hours results and Colette Kress's commentary will likely set the tone for AI/semiconductor sentiment into Wednesday, with options markets already pricing a large (~8.5%) move either way; Huang's comments about a potential return add a fresh variable to how the stock trades on the print.
- The ASX 200's sharpest fall in over a month, driven by miners and banks together, suggests both a domestic (hawkish RBA) and an imported (Wall Street, commodity prices) source of risk-off sentiment — a broader rotation away from risk assets rather than a single-sector story.
- Resilient US retail sales data alongside a hawkish Fed voice (Musalem) complicates the rate-cut narrative; confirmation of Tuesday's final US closing levels and Wednesday's market reaction to Nvidia will clarify whether this is a one-day wobble or the start of a deeper AI-led drawdown.
