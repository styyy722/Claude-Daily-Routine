## Key Financial News

- **Chip stocks slide:** The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index fell 2.64% to a three-week low; Nvidia -2.03%, Micron Technology -5.76%, Broadcom -1.74%, as investors positioned cautiously ahead of Nvidia's Wednesday earnings.
- **Iran sanctions escalate:** Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent announced "Operation Economic Outcast," expanding secondary sanctions on entities and countries maintaining business ties with Iran, as the Iran conflict nears its six-month mark.
- **US–Canada tariff dispute:** President Trump warned tariffs on Canadian cars, trucks and auto parts would rise to 50% from January 1 after weekend trade talks broke down, weighing on auto-sector sentiment.
- **Nvidia earnings loom:** Wednesday's report is seen as a pivotal test for the AI trade that has powered much of this year's tech rally.
- **ASX 200 gains on miners:** Materials, mining and resources stocks led Australian shares higher; Paladin Energy jumped 10.68% on the session.
- **Bonds and gold:** The 10-year Treasury yield eased to around 4.71%, helping gold hold firm near $4,630/oz amid ongoing Treasury buyback and geopolitical concerns.
- **Fed leadership transition:** Markets are looking ahead to the Jackson Hole Economic Symposium (Aug 27–29), the first under new Fed Chair Kevin Warsh, themed "Financial Innovation: Implications for Payments and Policy."

## Investment Implications

- Elevated geopolitical risk (Iran sanctions, US-Canada tariff tensions) is adding volatility to trade- and tariff-sensitive sectors, particularly autos and semiconductors.
- Nvidia's earnings on Wednesday could be a near-term catalyst for broader tech and AI-related equities in either direction.
- Australian resources and mining stocks are benefiting from firmer commodity sentiment, but broader index direction will likely take cues from Wall Street and upcoming Fed commentary at Jackson Hole.
