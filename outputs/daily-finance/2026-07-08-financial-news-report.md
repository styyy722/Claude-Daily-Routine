## Key Financial News

- **US-Iran Military Escalation:** US forces struck more than 80 targets in Iran overnight — including air defence systems, command centres, and radar sites — in response to attacks on three commercial vessels in the Strait of Hormuz. President Trump declared the US-Iran ceasefire memorandum "over." Iran threatened to close the Strait of Hormuz, raising fears of a sustained oil supply disruption. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/08/oil-prices-brent-wti-iran-us-hormuz.html))
- **Oil Surges to Multi-Year Highs:** WTI crude closed at $73.52/barrel (+4.4%) and Brent held near $78/barrel, hitting their highest levels since the early phase of the conflict. Treasury revoked Iran's oil export authorisation, further tightening supply. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/07/oil-prices-iran-strait-hormuz.html))
- **US Equities Mixed:** Dow fell ~500 points; S&P 500 slipped 0.2%. Energy names and defence-adjacent stocks outperformed — Broadcom +4.5%, Baker Hughes +4.1%, Nebius Group +3.8%. Tech-linked stocks with perceived geopolitical exposure sold off: Palo Alto Networks -5.2%, DoorDash -5.0%, Palantir -4.7%. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/live/stock-market-today-wednesday-july-8-dow-sp-nasdaq-fall-oil-rise-230111117.html))
- **Fed June Minutes — Divided on Rate Path:** The Federal Reserve's June meeting minutes revealed a divided committee; only a few policymakers favoured a rate increase, while most held rates steady. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh and colleagues flagged growing concern about inflation re-acceleration driven by the energy shock.
- **IMF Cuts Global Growth Outlook:** The International Monetary Fund modestly downgraded global growth forecasts, explicitly citing the energy shock triggered by the US-Iran conflict as a key headwind. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-july-8-2026))
- **ASX 200 Falls 1.2%:** The index closed at 8,699, with broad-based selling. BHP dropped 2.9%, Evolution Mining slid 3.8%, Telstra lost 3.1%, and Macquarie Group fell 2.4%. The four major banks declined 1.0–1.7%. Gold miners like Newmont and Northern Star faced headwinds despite gold's modest gain.
- **AUD/USD Holds Steady:** The Australian dollar was relatively resilient at 0.6933 (+0.06%), underpinned by rising commodity prices offsetting risk-off pressure.

## Investment Implications

- **Oil shock amplifies inflation risk globally:** If the Strait of Hormuz is disrupted, energy prices could spike far higher, threatening to reverse the disinflationary trends of 2024–25 and forcing central banks to delay or reverse rate cuts.
- **Energy sector outperforms; tech faces pressure:** The sharp rotation into energy names and away from high-multiple tech underscores vulnerability in an environment where rate-cut hopes fade. Australian energy producers (Beach Energy, Santos) may benefit.
- **ASX exposed via global risk-off and China trade:** With BHP and iron ore miners under pressure, Australian equities face a dual headwind — risk-off sentiment globally and any weakness in Chinese industrial demand that could weigh on the iron ore price.

## Watchlist for Today

- **Strait of Hormuz developments:** Any closure or further military action would be the single biggest driver for oil and global markets. Watch for US Central Command and State Department updates.
- **US CPI / Fed speakers:** With the June Fed minutes showing inflation concern, any further data or commentary from Warsh-era officials could sharpen the market's rate outlook.
- **ASX 200 open and energy/mining sector:** Watch energy stocks (BPT, STO) for follow-through from WTI's 4.4% gain and monitor whether the iron ore stabilisation ($98.86) supports a recovery in the major miners.
