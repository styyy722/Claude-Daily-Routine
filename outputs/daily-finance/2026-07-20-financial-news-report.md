# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-07-20

## Executive Summary
- US equities slipped again as escalating US-Iran tensions kept risk appetite in check; the S&P 500 (-0.19%) and Dow (-0.59%) fell while the Nasdaq was roughly flat (-0.05%) as chip stocks pared early gains.
- Oil jumped to a five-week high (WTI +0.9% to $83.23/bbl) after President Trump vowed Iran would "pay" for the deaths of US service members, with Strait of Hormuz shipping disruption still in focus.
- Hut 8 surged over 10% on a $9.8 billion AI data-center lease announcement, and Alphabet briefly rallied more than 3% on reports of a new internal AI chip, underscoring a market torn between geopolitical risk-off flows and AI-driven optimism ahead of a heavy earnings week (Tesla, Alphabet, Intel).

## Key Financial News

- **US Equities Mixed-to-Lower:** The S&P 500 fell 0.19% to 7,443.28 and the Dow dropped 0.59% (-307.16 pts) to 51,839.26, while the Nasdaq was nearly flat, down just 0.05% to 25,508.07, as investors weighed escalating US-Iran tensions against a heavy week of tech earnings. ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/07/20/stock-market-today-july-20-hut-8-surges-10-as-geopolitical-tensions-weigh-on-broader-market/))
- **Oil Climbs on Iran Escalation:** WTI crude advanced 0.9% to settle at $83.23/bbl, a five-week high, after President Trump said Iran would "pay" for killing US service members; fresh CENTCOM strikes and continued restrictions on Strait of Hormuz shipping kept energy markets on edge. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/20/oil-prices-today-brent-wti-crude-us-iran-centcom-hormuz.html))
- **Hut 8 Surges on AI Data-Center Deal:** Hut 8 jumped 10.37%, the market's top gainer, after announcing a $9.8 billion AI data-center lease, standing out against broader market weakness. ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/07/20/stock-market-today-july-20-hut-8-surges-10-as-geopolitical-tensions-weigh-on-broader-market/))
- **Chip Rally Fades, Alphabet Pares Gains:** AMD, Micron, Intel and Coherent all opened higher on AI-infrastructure optimism but gave back much of the move by the close; Alphabet rose more than 3% intraday on reports of a new internal AI chip before paring gains. ([The Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/07/20/stock-market-today-july-20-hut-8-surges-10-as-geopolitical-tensions-weigh-on-broader-market/))
- **Gold Holds Above $4,000:** Spot gold edged up 0.10% to $4,008.09/oz, holding above the psychological $4,000 level even as a stronger dollar and rising 10-year Treasury yields (~4.59%) limited safe-haven flows despite the active Iran conflict. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-monday-july-20-2026-gold-holds-above-4000-despite-continued-violence-in-the-middle-east-152926843.html))
- **ASX 200 Slips on IT and Healthcare Weakness:** Australia's benchmark eased 0.06% to 8,791.30 as losses in IT, utilities and healthcare outweighed a 6.16% jump in Yancoal Australia; PEXA Group was the worst performer, falling 4.68% to an all-time low. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/australia-stocks-lower-at-close-of-trade-spasx-200-down-006-4799859))
- **AUD/USD Firms Modestly:** The Australian dollar edged up 0.11% to around 0.6990 against the US dollar. ([TradingEconomics](https://tradingeconomics.com/australia/currency))
