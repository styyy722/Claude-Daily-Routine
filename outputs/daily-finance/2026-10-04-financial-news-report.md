## Key Financial News
- **OPEC+ holds November output steady**: The group's seven core members (Saudi Arabia, Russia, Iraq, Kuwait, Kazakhstan, Algeria and Oman) met virtually on Sunday and agreed to maintain September's required production levels for November 2026 — signalling no additional planned supply increase for the month. The group finished unwinding 1.65 million bpd of 2023 voluntary cuts in September and is keeping a further 2 million bpd of 2022-agreed cuts in place through year-end; it is also reviewing members' production capacity ahead of setting 2027 quotas. The next OPEC+ meeting is 1 November. ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/energy/2026/10/04/opec-keeps-oil-output-targets-unchanged-for-november/))
- **Markets closed for the weekend**: US, Australian and most global equity exchanges do not trade on Sundays; Friday 2 October's close remains the operative reference level heading into Monday.
- **Weak September jobs report still the dominant Fed story**: US nonfarm payrolls rose just 29,000 versus roughly 81,000–90,000 expected, with unemployment ticking up to 4.2%. Traders sharply cut October rate-hike odds, fuelling Friday's broad equity rally.
- **AI/chip strength lifted US indices into the close Friday**: Nvidia, Tesla and other mega-cap names extended gains, with Tesla up on a strong Q3 delivery beat (486,532 vehicles); this concentration in a narrow set of names remains a feature of the current rally.
- **ASX 200 extended its rebound into the weekend**: The index closed +0.79% at 8,682.10 Friday, continuing to recover from Thursday's steep drop; AUD/USD last reliably quoted around 0.6957 as markets continue digesting the RBA's 29 September rate hike to 4.60%.
- **Oil heads into Monday with the OPEC+ decision now resolved**: WTI closed Friday at $91.11/bbl (-1.90%); with November output confirmed unchanged, Monday's open will test whether the no-surprise decision is already priced in or triggers fresh positioning.
- **No fresh iron ore print available for the weekend**: No new Saturday or Sunday Qingdao/SGX 62% Fe quote was found; Friday's reference level (~$91.35/T) is carried forward rather than invented, per the no-invented-data rule.
