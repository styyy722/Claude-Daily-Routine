# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-10-01

## Executive Summary
- US equities eked out modest gains to start October (S&P 500 +0.19% to 7,666.45, Dow +0.04% to 50,926.56, Nasdaq Composite +0.04% to 26,871.60) as Treasury yields retreated from their highest levels in over two decades, though some sources still pegged the 10-year near a fresh ~24-year high intraday.
- The ASX 200 sold off sharply, -1.99% (-174.9pts) to 8,614.4 — its steepest one-day fall since early March and lowest level since mid-June — dragged down by Lynas Rare Earths (-5.6%) and Ampol (-2.4%) after both announced acquisitions, alongside fading optimism on further RBA easing.
- The US fiscal year-end (Oct 1) passed without a government shutdown, as the continuing resolution signed September 2 already funds the government through December 11.

## Key Financial News
- **Wall Street opens October with modest gains**: The S&P 500, Dow, and Nasdaq Composite all edged higher as Treasury yields pulled back from multi-decade highs intraday, clawing back earlier session declines. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/30/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-oct-01-2026))

([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/australia-stocks-lower-at-close-of-trade-spasx-200-down-199-4926337), [ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-10-01/asx-markets-business-news-live-updates-thursday-1-october/107204216))
- **Lynas Rare Earths agrees $968m scheme to acquire Meteoric Resources**: The all-share deal (28.6c/share, a 68.4% premium) would lift Lynas' measured and indicated rare earth oxide resources by ~79% and ore reserves by ~26% via Meteoric's Caldeira project in Brazil; Lynas shares nonetheless fell 5.6% on the announcement. ([Capital Brief](https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/lynas-rare-earths-buys-asx-listed-miner-meteoric-resources-in-968m-deal-a0b5fcbf-2d28-4c3a-8f7f-86d86a37798c/), [Stockhead](https://stockhead.com.au/news/lunch-wrap-asx-mugged-by-bond-yields-as-lynas-eats-meteoric-whole-for-968m/))
- **Ampol to acquire EV charging network Evie Networks for $225m**: Combined with its existing AmpCharge network, Ampol will operate roughly 1,425 charging bays across 400+ sites; Ampol shares slipped 2.4%. ([Capital Brief](https://www.capitalbrief.com/briefing/australian-shares-to-fall-after-late-selloff-on-wall-street-adb7f506-7ffc-456b-8238-73808c3a0e55/))
- **US fiscal year-end passes without shutdown drama**: FY2027 began October 1 without disruption, as the continuing resolution Congress passed on September 2 (signed into law, Senate 90-6, House 370-48) already funds the government through December 11. ([Congress.gov CRS report](https://www.congress.gov/crs-product/R48832))
- **Treasury yields remain near multi-decade highs despite intraday pullback**: Sources diverge on the exact 10-year print (5.24% vs. 5.33%), but both are near the highest levels since 2002, continuing to pressure rate-sensitive assets even as equities stabilised. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/article/10-year-treasury-yield-touches-highest-level-since-2002-alphacheck-135255038.html))

([Trading Economics – Gold](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/gold), [Trading Economics – Iron Ore](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/iron-ore))

## Investment Implications
- The divergence between resilient (if modest) US gains and a sharp ASX selloff highlights that local, stock-specific M&A reactions (Lynas, Ampol) and fading RBA-easing bets are currently driving Australian equities more than global risk sentiment.
- Persistently elevated long-end Treasury yields — regardless of which source's exact print is used — continue to be a headwind for valuation-sensitive and non-yielding assets (growth equities, gold); this dynamic bears watching into October regardless of near-term CPI/PCE surprises.
- M&A premiums being paid in the critical-minerals and EV-infrastructure space (Lynas/Meteoric, Ampol/Evie) suggest continued strategic consolidation in resources and energy-transition infrastructure, even as the acquirers' share prices react negatively in the near term.
