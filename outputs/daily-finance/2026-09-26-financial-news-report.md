## Key Financial News
- **Trump rejects Iran's seven-day Hormuz roadmap**: Iran formally submitted a plan via mediator Qatar on Friday to reopen the Strait of Hormuz and restart peace talks within seven days, conditional on the US lifting its naval blockade, waiving oil sanctions, releasing ~$12bn in frozen Iranian assets, and observing a regional ceasefire. Trump rejected it on Saturday, saying Iran is "losing so badly," though he left the door open to further talks. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/9/26/trump-rejects-irans-seven-day-roadmap-to-reopen-strait-of-hormuz), [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/national-security/2026/09/26/trump-rejects-iran-proposal-reopen-strait-hormuz-restart-peace-talks/))
- **Oil eased into the weekend before the rejection landed**: WTI fell 2.33% to $92.41/bbl and Brent dropped 2.14% to $104.32/bbl on Friday on profit-taking and easing (as it turned out, premature) supply-risk concerns; the Brent-WTI spread stayed wide (~$12), reflecting ongoing freight/logistics stress from the conflict. ([Rio Times Online](https://www.riotimesonline.com/oil-markets-latam-saturday-september-26-2026/))
- **Wall Street closed a volatile week with broad gains**: The S&P 500 (+0.51%), Dow (+0.93%) and Nasdaq Composite (+0.48%) all rose Friday even as the 10-year Treasury yield pushed to a fresh post-2007 high (~5.12–5.18%), as equity markets continued to treat the bond sell-off as an inflation-risk repricing rather than a growth scare. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-september-25-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-081738529.html))
- **ASX 200 slides to a more-than-three-month low ahead of the RBA**: The index fell to roughly 8,659–8,665 on Friday, its lowest close since 11 June and down nearly 5% over the past month, on broad-based weakness (155 of 200 constituents lower); resources led losses (BHP -0.66%, Rio Tinto -0.88%) while major banks were a relative bright spot (CBA +0.28%, Westpac +0.56%). ([The Motley Fool Australia](https://www.fool.com.au/2026/09/25/asx-200-sinks-to-june-lows-as-investors-brace-for-another-rba-rate-hike/))
- **RBA rate-hike odds firm further ahead of Tuesday's decision**: Money markets are now pricing a 90–94% probability of a 25bp hike to 4.60% at the 29 September meeting, with all four major banks (CBA, ANZ, NAB, Westpac) forecasting the move and ANZ still tipping a follow-up hike to 4.85% in November; traders see roughly 40bp of further tightening priced in by year-end. ([OrbitRemit](https://blog.orbitremit.com/rba-rate-decision-september-2026-preview/))
- **Gold and AUD/USD little changed in thin weekend trading**: Indicative pricing showed gold easing to around $4,285/oz from Friday's $4,321.60 close, while AUD/USD held broadly steady in the ~0.70–0.71 range with official FX markets largely closed for the weekend.

## Investment Implications
- Trump's rejection of Iran's Hormuz roadmap removes, for now, the de-escalation catalyst that had pushed oil lower on Friday; expect renewed upside risk to WTI/Brent and elevated volatility in energy-sensitive names when markets reopen Monday.
- Equities rallying into a fresh post-2007-high 10-year yield suggests markets remain willing to read the bond sell-off as inflation repricing rather than a growth scare, but that read is untested against a weekend geopolitical setback and remains vulnerable to a reversal.
- With RBA hike odds now at 90%+ and reinforced by four major banks, rate-sensitive ASX sectors (property, retail, financials) are likely to see continued volatility into and through Tuesday's decision.
