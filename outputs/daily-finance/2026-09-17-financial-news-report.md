# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-09-17

## Executive Summary
- US equities rallied sharply on Thursday — S&P 500 +1.14% to 7,637.76, Dow +0.61% to 51,778.04, Nasdaq Composite +1.69% to 26,418.30 — as falling oil prices eased inflation worries a day after the Fed's rate hike, with a closely watched chipmaker gauge up roughly 3%.

## Key Financial News
- **US stocks rebound the day after the Fed hike**: The S&P 500 rose 1.14% to 7,637.76, the Dow gained 0.61% (+316.14 pts) to 51,778.04, and the Nasdaq Composite jumped 1.69% to 26,418.30, as falling oil prices supported optimism that inflation can be kept under control. A closely watched gauge of chipmakers climbed roughly 3%. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-thursday-september-17-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-oil-fed-081248626.html))
- **10-year Treasury yield retreats from post-2007 high**: The benchmark yield eased to about 4.94%, down from an intraday peak of roughly 5.04% (the highest since 2007) reached a day earlier, snapping an eight-day rising streak as bond markets stabilised alongside equities.
- **Oil slides on Saudi pipeline restart news**: WTI fell 1.4% to ~$101.00/bbl (having dropped as much as 3.5% intraday to ~$102 in Asian trade, with Brent down 3% to ~$105.62) after reports that Saudi Arabia's damaged East-West pipeline would be restored soon, easing supply concerns. ([Fool.com.au](https://www.fool.com.au/2026/09/17/5-things-to-watch-on-the-asx-200-on-thursday-17-september-2026/))
- **ASX 200 rises 0.41%, led by Financials and Healthcare**: The index added 36 points to close at 8,732.2, extending Wednesday's advance despite SPI futures pointing to a ~0.7% weaker open overnight. Dyno Nobel (+8.0%) and DroneShield (+7.4%) led gainers; Ramelius Resources and Capricorn Metals lagged as gold miners softened. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/australia-stocks-higher-at-close-of-trade-spasx-200-up-041-4904850))
- **Gold eases as equity risk appetite improves**: Gold futures traded in a $4,300-$4,380/oz range through the session (COMEX futures closing near $4,381.30, -0.14%) as Wednesday's rate hike and improving equity sentiment reduced haven demand; ASX-listed gold miners Newmont and Northern Star traded subdued. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-price-today-thursday-september-17-2026-gold-prices-relatively-stable-following-fed-rate-increase-110852391.html), [Fool.com.au](https://www.fool.com.au/2026/09/17/5-things-to-watch-on-the-asx-200-on-thursday-17-september-2026/))
- **AUD/USD holds near 0.7109**: The Australian dollar traded around 0.7109, easing modestly from Wednesday's ~0.7130 level, as markets continued to digest the Fed's hawkish dot plot ahead of the RBA's own decision due September 29.
- **Iron ore softens to one-month lows in China, USD benchmark near $97/T**: The DCE most-traded contract eased toward one-month lows around 710.5 yuan/mt (+0.28% on the session) while the 62% Fe USD futures opened near $97.41/T; Qingdao spot prices were broadly flat amid thin trading and weakening Chinese steel demand. ([SMM](https://news.metal.com/newscontent/104120822-mmi-daily-iron-ore-report-september-17))

## Investment Implications
- Thursday's broad rally — led by a ~3% pop in chipmakers and a pullback in yields — suggests markets are reading the Fed's 25bp hike as a "one-and-done-for-now" move rather than the start of an aggressive tightening cycle, though 16 of 18 FOMC officials still project another hike this year.
- Falling oil prices on the Saudi pipeline restart news are easing near-term inflation pressure, helping equities and bonds rally together — a dynamic whose durability depends on whether the pipeline restart materialises as reported.
- Local investors face a similar tailwind: ASX 200 gains were broad-based across Financials and Healthcare, but softer gold and iron ore prices highlight ongoing headwinds for resource-heavy portfolios ahead of the RBA's September 29 decision.
