# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-09-02

## Executive Summary
- US equities snapped a three-day losing streak, with the S&P 500, Dow, and Nasdaq all rising as Treasury yields paused their recent climb despite the 10-year hitting a fresh multi-year high intraday.
- Fed September rate-hike odds jumped to roughly 66-68% (from ~40% a week earlier) following Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole remarks, even as a weaker-than-expected ADP private payrolls report (38,000 vs. 47,000 forecast) pointed to a cooling labor market.
- The ASX 200 fell nearly 1% to a 20-day low as the US-Iran conflict kept oil elevated and Australia's Q2 GDP data fueled speculation of an imminent RBA rate hike.

## Key Financial News

- **Wall Street rebounds after three-day slide:** The S&P 500 rose 0.46% to 7,666.60, the Dow gained 295.07 points (+0.56%) to 53,061.95, and the Nasdaq Composite added 0.45% to 26,217.83, as Treasury yields took a breather from their recent surge. The small-cap Russell 2000 outperformed, up 1.13%. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-2-134032621.html), [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-02-2026))
- **US-Iran conflict continues to drive sentiment:** Renewed hostilities — including a second round of US strikes near the Strait of Hormuz and Iranian retaliation — kept crude prices elevated, though some investors hoped for an "escalate to de-escalate" outcome from the Trump administration's approach. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-2-134032621.html))
- **10-year Treasury yield hits fresh multi-year high:** The benchmark yield touched roughly 4.81%, its highest level since late 2023, before stabilizing intraday; the pause in the yield climb helped equities rebound. ([Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/united-states/government-bond-yield))
- **Fed rate-hike odds jump to ~66-68% for September:** Markets sharply repriced the odds of a 25bp September hike, up from roughly 40% a week earlier, after Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole commentary on persistent inflation. ([Chase](https://www.chase.com/personal/investments/learning-and-insights/article/september-2026-rate-hike-now-expected-amid-energy-shocks), [TechTimes](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/326153/20260901/fed-rate-hike-68-odds-opens-five-day-window-variable-rate-borrowers.htm))
- **Labor market shows signs of cooling:** ADP reported private-sector payrolls grew by just 38,000 in August, below the 47,000 forecast and the weakest reading since January, adding a dovish counterweight to the hawkish Fed narrative. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-2-134032621.html))
- **Australia's economy loses momentum despite GDP beat:** June-quarter GDP grew 0.4% quarter-on-quarter (2.1% annually), which Treasurer Jim Chalmers called equal to the US and stronger than most advanced economies — but analysts noted the six-month annualised pace has slowed to around 1.5%, and the data fueled speculation (including from HSBC's chief economist) of a September RBA rate hike. Australia's 10-year borrowing costs hit their highest level since 2011. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-02/asx-markets-business-live-news/107105482))
- **ASX 200 falls to 20-day low:** The index dropped 88.3 points (-0.97%) to 8,978.40 as the global bond sell-off and elevated oil prices weighed on sentiment, despite the positive GDP print. AUD/USD slipped 0.21% to 0.7145, and Singapore iron ore fell 1.75% to $97.75/tonne. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-02/asx-markets-business-live-news/107105482))
- **Big tech earnings in focus:** Broadcom, Snowflake, and Hewlett Packard Enterprise reported quarterly results, adding to investor focus on AI-related capex and demand trends. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-2-134032621.html))

## Investment Implications

- The rebound in US equities alongside surging Fed rate-hike odds suggests markets are for now more focused on a pause in the yield spike than on the underlying hawkish repricing — a dynamic that could reverse quickly if yields resume climbing toward the mid-September FOMC meeting.
- The weak ADP print complicates the Fed's calculus: a softening labor market alongside persistent inflation pressure raises the risk of a policy misstep in either direction, keeping volatility elevated into the FOMC decision.
- In Australia, the combination of resilient (if slowing) GDP growth and rising RBA rate-hike speculation, alongside multi-decade-high domestic bond yields, points to a more challenging environment for rate-sensitive ASX sectors even as headline growth holds up.
