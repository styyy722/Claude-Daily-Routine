# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-08-29

## Executive Summary
- Saturday was a non-trading day for US and Australian equity markets; Friday's close remains the last print after Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole keynote pushed September rate-hike odds to a near coin flip.
- Warsh recommitted the Fed to a "firm, fixed target" of 2% PCE inflation, noting 12-month PCE inflation stood at 3.7% and 54% of the consumption basket was still rising faster than 3%.
- Fed funds futures and prediction markets (CME FedWatch, Kalshi) now price roughly a 48-57% chance of a 25-basis-point hike at the 15-16 September FOMC meeting, up sharply from about 35% before the speech.

## Key Financial News

- **Warsh's Jackson Hole keynote reprices September odds:** In his first Jackson Hole address as Fed Chair, Kevin Warsh said inflation is still too high and signalled the Fed may need to raise rates further, framing price stability as the central bank's "predominant" focus. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-aug-28-2026))
- **Rate-hike odds jump to a coin flip:** Odds of a September hike surged to roughly 48-57% (CME FedWatch, Kalshi) from about 35% before the speech, after previously sitting near 70% odds of a hold. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/-september-fed-decision-now-a-coin-flip-as-rate-hike-odds-increase.html))
- **Treasury yields jump, dollar strengthens:** The 2-year yield touched its highest level since July at around 4.32%, the 10-year rose to about 4.72%, and the US dollar index climbed roughly 0.50% to 99.65 on the hawkish repricing.
- **Gold retreats from three-month high:** Bullion slid about 3% Friday to roughly $4,454-4,455/oz as the dollar strengthened, giving back part of a rally that had earlier been fuelled by concerns over US Treasury debt buybacks and dollar debasement. ([Trading Economics](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/gold))
- **Wall Street still closes out a winning week:** Despite Friday's pullback, the S&P 500 rose 0.5% and the Nasdaq gained 0.9% for the week, while the Dow posted its first weekly gain in three weeks.
- **ASX 200 and AUD firm on local rate-hike bets:** Australia's benchmark gained 0.60% to a Friday close of 9,092.30, and the Australian dollar touched its highest level since May, as this week's hot core CPI print builds the case for the RBA to consider a hike by its 28-29 September meeting. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-28/asx-markets-business-news-live-updates/107087998))
- **Iron ore firm into the weekend:** Iron ore (CNY-denominated) rose 0.91% in its latest print to around 723 CNY/tonne, though it remains down over the past month on soft Chinese steel demand.

## Investment Implications

- Warsh's hawkish framing keeps a September rate hike squarely in play, and with the decision now priced as close to a coin flip, expect elevated volatility in rate-sensitive assets (tech, gold, Treasuries) into the 15-16 September FOMC meeting.
- The dollar's strength and gold's pullback from three-month highs suggest the debasement/buyback narrative that drove bullion higher earlier in August may need fresh catalysts to resume, with Fed policy clarity now the dominant driver.
- Diverging central bank paths — a Fed weighing a hike alongside an RBA also facing upside inflation risk ahead of its own September meeting — could keep AUD/USD and cross-asset volatility elevated into next month.
