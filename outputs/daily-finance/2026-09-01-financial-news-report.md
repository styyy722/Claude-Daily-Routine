# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-09-01

## Executive Summary
- US equities fell sharply Tuesday as a second round of US strikes on Iran near the Strait of Hormuz sent oil prices surging further and deepened a global bond sell-off, with the Dow dropping over 400 points.
- The 10-year Treasury yield jumped to 4.79% (from 4.73% Friday), its highest since January 2025, as bond yields also surged in Japan, Germany, and the UK on synchronized global inflation fears.
- Gold slid over 2% to below $4,450/oz — a two-week low — as rising oil prices and hawkish Fed rate-hike expectations (~60% odds for September, per CME FedWatch) outweighed safe-haven demand.

## Key Financial News

- **US strikes Iran again near Strait of Hormuz:** A second round of US strikes targeted Iranian capability to launch missiles and lay sea mines in the Strait of Hormuz, marking renewed combat after roughly a month; Iran struck back at US bases in Jordan. The escalation reignited fears of disruption to a key global oil and LNG chokepoint. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/31/stock-market-today-live-updates.html), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/stock-market-today-sept-1-135016820.html))
- **US equities close broadly lower:** The S&P 500 fell 0.71% to 7,631.47, the Dow lost 419.02 points (-0.79%) to 52,766.88, and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.03% to 26,099.77, as rising bond yields and oil prices weighed on risk sentiment. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-september-1-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-080617884.html), [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/coverage/stock-market-today/2026/09/01/stock-market-midday-sept-1-stocks-slide-on-global-bond-sell-off/))
- **Global bond sell-off accelerates:** The 10-year Treasury yield rose to 4.79% from 4.73% Friday, its highest since January 2025. The sell-off was global: Japan's 10-year yield touched 3% for the first time since 1996, Germany's 10-year hit a 15-year high near 3%, and the UK 10-year gilt yield jumped to 5.23%, its highest since the global financial crisis. ([CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/01/investing/global-bond-market), [Irish Times](https://www.irishtimes.com/business/2026/09/01/bond-sell-off-accelerates-globally-amid-inflation-fears/))
- **Fed rate-hike odds hold near 60%:** CME FedWatch showed roughly a 60% chance of a quarter-point September hike, nearly double pre-Jackson Hole odds, after Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's hawkish remarks that "underlying trends have not meaningfully improved" on inflation. Some prediction markets (e.g. Kalshi) showed a closer split near 48-52%. ([Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/investing/2026/09/01/odds-sept-rate-hike-doubled-fed-chair-kevin-warsh/), [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/28/-september-fed-decision-now-a-coin-flip-as-rate-hike-odds-increase.html))
- **Oil extends gains on Hormuz risk:** WTI climbed above $86/bbl and neared $90 intraday as renewed US-Iran hostilities raised fresh concerns over disruption to Strait of Hormuz energy flows, extending Monday's rally. ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/gold-collapses-as-us-iran-strikes-send-oil-us-yields-higher-202609011930))
- **Gold slides to two-week low:** Bullion fell more than 2.3% to below $4,450/oz as the combination of a stronger dollar, higher yields, and hawkish Fed pricing outweighed the usual geopolitical safe-haven bid. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-tuesday-september-1-2026-gold-moves-lower-as-rate-hike-expectations-grow-120810063.html))
- **ASX 200 slips as house prices fall:** The ASX 200 closed down 0.10% at 9,066, weighed by a continuing domestic housing slump alongside the broader risk-off tone from oil prices and the US-Iran escalation. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-01/asx-markets-business-live-news-house-prices-fall/107100764))
- **Iron ore holds steady:** The 62% Fe benchmark held near $95.84/tonne, up 0.17%, with China's demand described as restocking-driven rather than a strong pickup in end-user steel consumption, keeping the broader $93-100 range intact. ([Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/iron-ore-markets-latam-tuesday-september-1-2026/))

## Investment Implications

- The combination of a renewed oil shock, a synchronized global bond sell-off, and rising Fed rate-hike odds (~60%) raises stagflation-style risk for equities and rate-sensitive assets heading into the mid-September FOMC meeting.
- Global yield pressure — not just US Treasuries but also Japanese, German, and UK bonds at multi-decade or multi-year highs — suggests this is a broader inflation and fiscal-sustainability repricing rather than a US-specific event, which could keep volatility elevated across asset classes.
- Gold's failure to rally despite an active Middle East conflict signals that dollar strength and rate expectations are currently dominating over safe-haven demand; a reversal in Fed rate-hike odds could quickly change that dynamic.
