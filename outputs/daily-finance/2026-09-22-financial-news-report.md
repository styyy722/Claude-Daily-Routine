# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-09-22

## Executive Summary
- US equities were mixed but the Nasdaq Composite notched a fresh record close (+0.45% to 27,244.28) as AI/chip stocks rallied and falling oil eased inflation worries, while the S&P 500 finished essentially flat (7,764.64) and the Dow slipped 0.36% to 51,863.69, with markets positioning ahead of the Trump-Xi summit in Washington (Xi arriving Sept 23, leaders meeting Sept 24).
- The ASX 200 rose 0.3% to 8,758, led by technology (Megaport +4.3%, NextDC +3.8%) and select miners, while energy names lagged (Origin Energy -4%, Woodside -2.4%) after RBA Governor Michele Bullock said AI data-centre construction is "adding" to Australia's demand-driven inflation pressures, and CBA brought forward its rate-hike forecast to September.
- Oil rebounded modestly after four straight down sessions (WTI ~$92.88/bbl, +0.55%; Brent ~$101.58-102.09/bbl, +1.2-1.7%), while gold eased intraday to around $4,322/oz (-0.5%) as elevated Treasury yields (10-yr ~4.95%) and a firm US dollar weighed on bullion.

## Key Financial News
- **Nasdaq sets fresh record as AI/chip stocks rally; Dow and S&P 500 diverge**: The Nasdaq Composite rose 0.45% to a record close of 27,244.28 on continued AI-driven chip strength, while the S&P 500 was essentially unchanged at 7,764.64 (-0.06 pts) and the Dow fell 185.14 pts (-0.36%) to 51,863.69, as falling oil prices eased inflation concerns even as some cyclical and energy-linked names lagged. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-22-2026), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-tuesday-september-22-nasdaq-dow-sp-500-080625961.html))
- **Markets position ahead of Trump-Xi summit**: Chinese President Xi Jinping is due in Washington on September 23 ahead of a one-day summit with President Trump on September 24, with trade truce extension, tariffs, AI, critical minerals and the Iran war all on the agenda; Beijing reportedly wants the truce extended through the end of Trump's term while US Trade Representative Jamieson Greer has floated three to six months. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/21/trump-xi-china-summit-trade-tariffs.html), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/xi-trump-summit-agenda-ai-tariffs-critical-minerals-iran-war-taiwan))
- **ASX 200 gains 0.3% on tech and select miners; RBA flags AI-driven inflation risk**: The ASX 200 closed at 8,758, led by Megaport (+4.3%), NextDC (+3.8%) and Life360 (+2.9%), plus mining standouts Sunrise Energy Metals (+12.7%), FireFly Metals (+5.7%) and Bellevue Gold (+4.8%); Energy lagged (Origin Energy -4%, Woodside Energy -2.4%, AGL Energy -2.3%). RBA Governor Michele Bullock said AI data-centre construction is adding to demand-driven inflation and that unemployment may need to sit around 4.5-5% to ease price pressures; CBA brought forward its RBA rate-hike forecast to September (from November). ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-22/asx-markets-business-live-news/107179814))
- **10-year Treasury yield holds near 4.95% ahead of fresh jobs data**: US Treasury yields were roughly flat as investors awaited new economic data and Fed commentary, with the 10-year holding just above the 4.95% level reached after last week's post-2007 highs. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/amp/2026/09/22/treasury-yield-us-bond-market-trump.html))
- **Oil rebounds after four-session slide**: WTI rose about 0.55% to roughly $92.88/bbl and Brent gained between 1.2% and 1.7% to a range of about $101.58-102.09/bbl, snapping a four-day losing streak even as diplomatic efforts around the Middle East conflict and steadier regional energy flows kept a lid on further gains. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-22-2026))
- **Gold eases as yields and dollar strength persist; Chinese demand highlighted**: Spot gold traded in a wide intraday range (roughly $4,307-4,359/oz) and eased on the day to around $4,322/oz (-0.5%) as elevated Treasury yields and a firm US dollar continued to weigh on bullion, even as Yahoo Finance highlighted record Chinese gold import demand as a supportive longer-term factor. ([Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/gold-silver-precious-metals-tuesday-september-22-2026), [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-price-today-tuesday-september-22-2026-gold-prices-holding-as-chinese-gold-imports-set-record-104326063.html))
- **Iron ore steady as Chinese steel output rises**: Singapore iron ore futures settled near $97.05/T (+$0.05), while the Dalian (DCE) contract eased 0.07% to about 715 yuan/tonne; CISA data showed daily crude steel output at large Chinese mills up 2.0% versus late August, providing a price floor even as oversupply warnings capped further gains. ([Rio Times](https://www.riotimesonline.com/iron-ore-markets-latam-tuesday-september-22-2026/))

## Investment Implications
- The Nasdaq's fresh record alongside a flat S&P 500 and a softer Dow points to continued rotation toward AI/tech leadership rather than a broad-based rally, leaving market breadth in focus heading into the Trump-Xi summit.
- With the 10-year yield holding near 4.95% and the Fed's tightening path still a live debate, rate-sensitive assets (gold, growth equities) may stay range-bound until fresh jobs data or Fed commentary provides clearer direction.
- RBA Governor Bullock's comments linking AI infrastructure spending to inflation, together with CBA's earlier rate-hike call, reinforce the near-unanimous market expectation for a 25bp RBA hike on September 29 and keep AUD and rate-sensitive ASX sectors in focus.
