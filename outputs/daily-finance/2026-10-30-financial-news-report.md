- US equities suffered their worst session in months as a wave of disappointing Big Tech earnings reactions (Amazon, Intel, Reddit, ServiceNow, Fortinet) and a hawkish Fed hangover drove the Nasdaq Composite down 2.86%, its steepest drop since early June, with October closing as the Nasdaq's worst month since March 2025.

## Key Financial News
- **Big Tech earnings reaction rout**: A cluster of disappointing post-earnings reactions hit the market — Intel plunged ~28.6% (its worst day since 1974) on a weak Q4 outlook despite a profit beat; Reddit fell sharply (reports range ~24–29%) on soft user growth; ServiceNow dropped ~27% on weak RPO guidance; Fortinet fell ~20% despite beating estimates, on a cut full-year outlook; Roblox fell ~16–18% on a DAU decline; and Amazon fell (sources range ~2–4%) after flagging a record negative trailing-12-month free cash flow (over $42B outflow) and more than doubled YoY Q3 capex (~$77B).
- **Fed holds rates after hawkish split vote**: The Federal Reserve's October 27–28 FOMC meeting held the federal funds target range at 3.75%–4.00%, with Governor Lisa Cook and Cleveland Fed President Beth Hammack both dissenting in favor of a hike. Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference and subsequent hawkish remarks from St. Louis Fed's Alberto Musalem and Richmond Fed's Tom Barkin reinforced the case that rates may need to rise further to curb inflation; markets were pricing roughly 85% odds of a 25bp hike at the December 9 meeting following the decision.
- **Crypto selloff on Binance security breach**: Binance disclosed a security incident with an estimated ~$6.3B in losses (~$3B frozen on-chain), reportedly tied to a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop; the exchange said user funds are safe and it will cover losses. Bitcoin fell to a six-week low (~$77,646) and crypto-linked equities — Coinbase, Robinhood, Strategy (formerly MicroStrategy), Circle — each fell more than 6%.
- **Volatility spike**: The VIX jumped roughly 13% to ~19.4 (one source cited a lower, inconsistent ~17.2 level), consistent with the broad risk-off tone across equities and crypto.
- **ASX moves on earnings and outage, ahead of RBA**: Coles shares fell ~7% after a disappointing Q1 sales update flagged by RBC's Michael Toner as signaling market-share losses and a possible H1 EBIT downgrade; CommSec reported a Friday-morning trading outage. Markets are pricing roughly a 90% chance of a 25bp RBA hike to 4.85% at the November 3 decision, with all four major Australian banks expecting a hike and HSBC flagging a likely pause thereafter; this follows confirmation of annual inflation running at 4.3%.
- **Bank of Japan holds, yen firms**: The BoJ held its policy rate at 1.25%, and the yen firmed to around 157.1 per US dollar.
- **October wrap**: For the month, the S&P 500 finished down ~1.2%, the Dow rose ~1.5%, and the Nasdaq Composite fell ~5.2–5.3% — its worst month since March 2025 — underscoring how concentrated this week's Big Tech-earnings-driven selloff was in large-cap growth/tech names.

## Investment Implications
- The breadth of earnings-reaction casualties (Intel, Reddit, ServiceNow, Fortinet, Roblox, Amazon) alongside October's outsized Nasdaq underperformance suggests investors are punishing even modest guidance misses severely after a long run-up, rather than this being driven by one or two isolated stock stories.
- The Fed's hawkish hold with two dissents favoring a hike, combined with elevated Treasury yields, signals the easing narrative many investors had priced in may be further off than expected — a dynamic that could keep pressure on richly valued growth stocks if it persists.
- The Binance breach is a reminder that crypto-market-specific operational risk can spill into equities with crypto exposure; investors with such exposure may want to watch for further details on fund recovery and any regulatory response.
