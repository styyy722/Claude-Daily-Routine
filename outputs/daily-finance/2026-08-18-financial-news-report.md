# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-08-18

## Executive Summary
- US stocks fell further from record highs as a broad chip-stock sell-off (PHLX Semiconductor Index -5%) hit Nvidia, Intel, Micron and memory makers, while the 30-year Treasury yield hit its highest level in nearly two decades.
- The S&P 500 dropped 0.69% to 7,691.76 and the Nasdaq Composite tumbled 1.33% to 26,289.71, with the Dow more resilient at -0.22%, helped by a strong Home Depot earnings beat.
- The ASX 200 finished essentially flat at 9,070 (-0.04%) as bumper earnings from BHP, CSL and Pro Medicus offset broader caution, narrowly missing a break from its five-day losing streak.

## Key Financial News

- **Chip stocks sink:** The PHLX Semiconductor Index dropped about 5%, with Nvidia down over 2%, Intel down 6%, Micron down 5%, and memory makers Sandisk and SK Hynix each down about 6%, dragging the Nasdaq sharply lower.
- **30-year Treasury yield hits multi-decade high:** The 30-year yield held near 5.29–5.32%, its highest level in nearly two decades, while the 10-year yield stayed elevated around 4.70–4.73% amid persistent concerns over inflation and US fiscal sustainability.
- **Indices diverge:** The S&P 500 fell 0.69% to 7,691.76 and the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.33% to 26,289.71, while the Dow held up better, down just 0.22% to 53,343.40, helped by strength in non-tech names; the small-cap Russell 2000 fell 1.30% to 3,017.89.
- **Home Depot beats:** Home Depot topped Q2 expectations with $47.9 billion in revenue and adjusted EPS of $4.79, providing a bright spot among consumer-facing names.
- **Klarna slides:** Klarna shares plunged after the buy-now-pay-later company trimmed its sales-related guidance.
- **Alphabet taps AUD debt market:** Alphabet launched an Australian-dollar bond sale, reflecting continued heavy financing needs tied to elevated AI infrastructure spending.
- **ASX earnings season in full swing:** The ASX 200 closed nearly flat at 9,070 (-0.04%) as bumper results from BHP, CSL and Pro Medicus offset broader market caution, with the index narrowly failing to snap its five-day losing streak.

## Investment Implications

- The chip-stock sell-off suggests AI-related equities remain vulnerable to sharp pullbacks even as underlying capital spending on AI infrastructure continues, creating a split market between mega-cap AI winners and more volatile semiconductor names.
- The 30-year Treasury yield's rise to a near two-decade high signals mounting investor concern over long-run US fiscal sustainability, a headwind that could keep valuations for long-duration and rate-sensitive assets under pressure.
- Strong individual earnings (Home Depot, BHP, CSL, Pro Medicus) show company-specific fundamentals can still offset macro and sector-wide headwinds, reinforcing the importance of stock selection in the current environment.
