## Key Financial News
- **Wall Street closes out a volatile week with broad gains**: The S&P 500 (+0.51%), Dow (+0.93%) and Nasdaq Composite (+0.48%) all rose, with the Dow snapping a three-week losing streak, as investors weighed "macroeconomic and geopolitical crosscurrents" following a sharp bond-yield sell-off earlier in the week. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-september-25-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-081738529.html))
- **10-year Treasury yield extends to a fresh post-2007 high**: The benchmark yield rose to roughly 5.18%, continuing a multi-session sell-off; one source described the move as "not a crisis but an eye-opener" for markets pricing renewed inflation risk. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/treasury-yields-bonds-debt.html))
- **Oil eases on Strait of Hormuz de-escalation hopes**: WTI fell roughly 2% (to the low-$90s/bbl) and Brent dropped below $98/bbl amid optimism over potential US-Iran negotiations to reopen the Strait of Hormuz. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-september-25-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-081738529.html))
- **Consumer sentiment falls to a four-month low**: The University of Michigan's consumer sentiment index dropped to 48.1 as households cited rising gas prices and tariff concerns, a soft data point that contrasted with the day's equity rally. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-september-25-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-081738529.html))
- **Meta rallies on AI enthusiasm; Nike downgraded; Costco beats**: Meta shares jumped about 13% on excitement over its new "Muse" AI agent; Bank of America downgraded Nike to Underperform citing "rising risks" to its turnaround; Costco topped earnings estimates (EPS $6.75 vs. $6.53 expected). ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-september-25-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-081738529.html))
- **ASX 200 hits a fresh 50-day low ahead of the RBA decision**: The index fell 0.43% to 8,665, now down 6.5% from its August peak (though roughly flat year-to-date), as investors positioned for Tuesday's RBA meeting; markets are pricing a 25bp hike, with Commonwealth Bank expecting a unanimous vote. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-25/asx-markets-business-news-live-updates-sept-25/107184538))

## Investment Implications
- Equities rallying even as the 10-year yield hits a fresh post-2007 high suggests markets are, for now, treating the bond sell-off as an inflation-risk repricing rather than a growth scare — but a weakening consumer (four-month-low sentiment) is a data point that could challenge that read if it persists.
- A sustained Strait of Hormuz de-escalation would remove a key geopolitical risk premium from oil prices, which could ease inflation pressure on both US and Australian consumers, including the petrol-price concerns flagged by local economists.
- With markets and Commonwealth Bank both leaning toward a 25bp RBA hike on Tuesday, rate-sensitive ASX sectors (property, retail, financials) are likely to stay volatile into the decision.
