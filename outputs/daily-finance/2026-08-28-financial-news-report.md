# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-08-28

## Executive Summary
- US stocks fell Friday after Fed Chair Kevin Warsh's first Jackson Hole keynote struck a hawkish tone, saying "inflation is running too high, price stability comes first," which reversed an early-session rally and pushed September rate-hike odds up sharply.
- Despite Friday's pullback, all three major US indexes closed out a winning week, with the Dow notching its first weekly gain in three weeks.
- The ASX 200 rose 0.60% to 9,092.30 and the Australian dollar hit its highest level since May, as RBA rate-hike bets built following this week's hot core CPI print.

## Key Financial News

- **Warsh strikes a hawkish note at Jackson Hole:** In his first keynote address as Fed Chair at the annual Jackson Hole Symposium, Kevin Warsh said "while this summer's PCE and CPI readings were better than expected, they do not tell me that underlying trends have meaningfully improved," and that price stability remains the Fed's predominant focus, reversing an early ~0.5% market rally into a decline.
- **Rate-hike odds jump:** Traders' odds of a 25-basis-point rate hike at the September Fed meeting surged to roughly 50-57%, up sharply from about 35-40% a day earlier; the 2-year Treasury yield posted its largest single-day jump since June.
- **Winning week despite Friday pullback:** The S&P 500 rose 0.5% for the week, the Nasdaq gained 0.9%, and the Dow advanced 0.5% — its first winning week in three — even after Friday's hawkish-driven reversal.
- **Nvidia gives back post-earnings gains:** Nvidia fell about 4.58% Friday, surrendering much of Thursday's 8.4% earnings-driven surge, as the semiconductor sector broadly declined (iShares Semiconductor ETF -3.20%).
- **Apple outperforms:** Apple shares rose about 1.63% after the company announced price increases for Apple TV and Apple One services.
- **Safe havens slip on hawkish pivot:** Gold and Bitcoin both reversed early gains to fall around 2.9% intraday after Warsh's remarks, though gold still held near $4,600-4,631/oz.
- **Australia's rate story firms:** The ASX 200 gained 0.60% to 9,092.30 and the Australian dollar climbed to its highest level since May, as this week's hotter-than-expected core CPI reading builds the case for an earlier RBA rate hike.

## Investment Implications

- Warsh's hawkish Jackson Hole message signals the Fed is unlikely to validate market expectations for near-term rate cuts, keeping a September hike squarely in play and adding volatility risk to rate-sensitive assets.
- The sharp reversal in Nvidia and broader semiconductors after Thursday's earnings-driven rally shows AI-related equities remain highly sensitive to macro rate signals even amid strong fundamentals.
- Diverging central bank narratives — a hawkish Fed alongside a Reserve Bank of Australia also facing upside inflation pressure — could keep AUD/USD and cross-asset volatility elevated into September.
