## Key Financial News
- **Iran holds firm after Trump's Hormuz rejection**: Foreign Minister Abbas Araghchi said Sunday that Iran "will not soften" the conditions in its seven-day roadmap (lifting the US naval blockade, waiving oil sanctions, releasing ~$12bn in frozen assets, and a regional ceasefire in exchange for reopening the Strait of Hormuz), and that Trump's Saturday rejection had not yet been formally relayed back through mediators Qatar and Pakistan. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-27/iran-says-won-t-soften-demands-after-trump-rejects-hormuz-offer), [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/us/2026/09/27/iran-seven-day-plan-araghchi/))
- **Oil remains volatile after Thursday's Houthi strikes on Saudi facilities**: Brent spiked more than 3% to above $106.50/bbl (briefly topping $108) on Thursday after Houthi missile and drone attacks on a target in Riyadh and Aramco facilities in Yanbu, before easing to around $105.77/bbl in Asian trade Friday and to a $104.32 Friday close; WTI settled Friday at $92.41/bbl (-2.33%), with indicative Sunday pricing near $91.35/bbl. ([Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/9/25/oil-prices-jump-after-yemens-houthis-claim-attacks-on-saudi-facilities))
- **Wall Street closed a volatile week with broad gains**: The S&P 500 (+0.51%), Dow (+0.93%) and Nasdaq Composite (+0.48%) all rose Friday even as the 10-year Treasury yield held at a fresh post-2007 high (~5.12–5.18%), with Akamai (+3%) a standout on a multiyear deal with Anthropic. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-september-25-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-081738529.html))
- **ASX 200 logs a fourth consecutive weekly decline**: The index fell 0.4% to close at 8,665 on Friday, its lowest level since 11 June, as investors stayed cautious ahead of Tuesday's RBA decision; resources led losses while the major banks were comparatively resilient. ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/capital-market-news/asx-200-falls-for-fourth-straight-week-ahead-of-rba-decision-126092500824_1.html))
- **RBA hike odds remain elevated into Tuesday's decision**: Money markets price a 90–94% probability of a 25bp hike to 4.60% at the 29 September meeting, with all four major domestic banks forecasting the move and ANZ still tipping a follow-up hike to 4.85% by year-end. ([OrbitRemit](https://blog.orbitremit.com/rba-rate-decision-september-2026-preview/))
- **AUD/USD and gold little changed over the weekend**: AUD/USD closed Friday at 0.7027 (+0.22%), while gold held broadly around $4,280/oz in thin weekend trading with official markets closed.

## Investment Implications
- Iran's public refusal to soften terms, combined with the still-fresh memory of Thursday's Houthi strikes, keeps upside risk to Brent/WTI alive into Monday's Asia-Pacific open; energy-sensitive sectors may see elevated volatility.
- Equities rallying into a fresh post-2007-high 10-year yield suggests markets are still treating the bond sell-off as an inflation-risk repricing rather than a growth scare, but that read has not yet been tested against a full trading session incorporating the weekend's unresolved Hormuz standoff.
- With RBA hike odds at 90%+ and a fourth straight weekly decline already behind the ASX 200, rate-sensitive sectors (property, retail, financials) are likely to stay volatile into and through Tuesday's decision.
