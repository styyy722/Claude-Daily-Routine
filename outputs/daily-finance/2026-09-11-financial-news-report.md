# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-09-11

## Executive Summary
- US equities snapped a four-day losing streak: the S&P 500 rose 0.86% to 7,656.98, the Dow gained 0.98%/+509.19pts to 52,573.29, and the Nasdaq Composite jumped 0.96% to 26,333.04, as falling oil prices offset a hotter-than-expected core CPI reading.
- August CPI rose 0.4% month-on-month (3.4% y/y), with core CPI up 0.3% m/m (2.4% y/y) — about 0.1pp above forecast — pushing Fed rate-hike odds for next week's meeting up to roughly 90% from about 72% the day before; the 10-year Treasury yield touched an intraday high of 4.954% (highest since October 2023) before easing to finish at ~4.91%.
- The ASX 200 fell to a fresh one-month low of 8,741.20 (-0.89%), dragged down by gold miners (Evolution Mining -3.8%, Northern Star -3.6%) and BHP (-4.1%) as gold slid to its lowest level in over a month, while the big four banks bucked the trend, up 0.2%–1.4%.

- **US stocks end losing streak on soft oil, in-line headline CPI**: The S&P 500 gained 0.86% to 7,656.98, the Dow rose 509.19 points (+0.98%) to 52,573.29, and the Nasdaq Composite added 0.96% to 26,333.04, as easing oil prices helped offset stickier-than-expected core inflation. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-september-11-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-cpi-inflation-082201751.html))

([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-11-2026))
- **Fed rate-hike odds jump to ~90%**: Futures markets now price roughly a 90% chance of a quarter-point Fed hike at next week's meeting, up from about 72% on Thursday, as investors weigh sticky core inflation against Middle East-driven energy price pressure. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/11/us-treasurys-bonds-yields.html))
- **10-year Treasury yield touches highest since October 2023**: The yield spiked intraday to 4.954% before easing to finish about 3.4 basis points lower at ~4.91%, reflecting volatile positioning around the CPI report and hike expectations. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/11/us-treasurys-bonds-yields.html))
- **ASX 200 hits fresh one-month low**: The index fell 78.20 points (-0.89%) to 8,741.20 as gold miners and BHP (-4.1%) led broad declines, though the big four banks rose 0.2%–1.4%, cushioning the fall. ([Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/australia-stocks-lower-at-close-of-trade-spasx-200-down-089-4897253))

## Investment Implications
- Markets are now pricing a Fed hike as highly likely (~90%) despite in-line headline inflation, showing investors are focused on the hotter core reading rather than the topline number — a dynamic that could keep yields and rate-sensitive sectors volatile into next week's decision.
- Gold's slide to a one-month low alongside rising hike odds illustrates the metal's sensitivity to real-yield expectations; further Fed hawkishness could extend the pullback, while any dovish surprise could quickly reverse it.
- The divergence between ASX gold miners/BHP (falling) and the big banks (rising) suggests investors are rotating within the index toward more rate-resilient, defensive names as commodity-linked stocks absorb the gold and iron ore weakness.
