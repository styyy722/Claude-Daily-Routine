# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-09-23

## Executive Summary
- US equities sold off sharply as the 10-year Treasury yield spiked to its highest level since 2007 (~5.11-5.14%), driven by a strong September flash PMI reading and hawkish Fed commentary; the S&P 500 fell 0.75% to 7,706.03, the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.13% to 26,936.04, and the Dow lost 0.68% to 51,511.59.
- The ASX 200 finished essentially flat (~8,765) as investors digested soft September flash PMIs and awaited Thursday's Australian labour market report, while RBA rate-hike expectations for the September 29 decision remained firm.
- Oil snapped a five-day losing streak (WTI +1.8% to $92.16/bbl, Brent +3.9% to $103.08/bbl) after President Trump described US-Iran talks as "very good," even as gold slipped roughly 1-1.5% toward $4,280-$4,300/oz on hawkish Fed signals and dollar strength.

## Key Financial News
- **US equities slide as 10-year Treasury yield hits highest level since 2007**: The S&P 500 fell 0.75% to 7,706.03, the Nasdaq Composite dropped 1.13% to 26,936.04 (hit hardest, alongside the Russell 2000), and the Dow lost 0.68% to 51,511.59, as the 10-year Treasury yield surged to roughly 5.11-5.14% following a stronger-than-expected September flash PMI reading that reignited inflation concerns. ([CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/23/investing/us-bond-market-fed), [TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-23-2026))
- **Fed officials strike a hawkish tone**: Fed Governor Michael Barr said "further policy adjustments are likely to be needed to ensure inflation comes down to target in a timely fashion," with traders pricing roughly 71% odds of another 25bp Fed hike at the October FOMC meeting.
- **Xi Jinping arrives in Washington ahead of Trump-Xi summit**: Chinese President Xi Jinping landed in Washington on September 23 ahead of the September 24 summit with President Trump; Xi arrived with a smaller-than-expected business delegation, seen by some observers as reflecting limited progress on deals ahead of talks covering AI, tariffs, critical minerals, the Iran war and Taiwan. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-22/xi-trump-summit-agenda-ai-tariffs-critical-minerals-iran-war-taiwan))
- **ASX 200 ends flat as investors await labour data**: The ASX 200 closed essentially unchanged around 8,765 as investors weighed soft September flash PMI readings (weaker services activity, continued manufacturing softness) and looked ahead to Thursday's Australian labour market report. ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/markets/capital-market-news/australia-s-asx-200-ends-flat-as-investors-await-key-economic-data-126092300811_1.html))
- **RBA rate-hike expectations firm ahead of September 29 decision**: The cash rate remains at 4.35% with July annual inflation at 3.5% (trimmed mean 3.6%); CommBank and Westpac economists expect a 25bp hike to 4.60% at the RBA's September 29 meeting, while ANZ expects two hikes taking the cash rate to 4.85%. ([Aussie.com.au](https://www.aussie.com.au/insights/news/expert-predictions-rba-rates/))
- **AUD/USD slides toward 0.7100 on broad US dollar strength**: The Australian dollar weakened roughly 0.15% to around 0.7100-0.7104, pressured by hawkish Fed commentary and firm US inflation-risk signals boosting the greenback broadly. ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/aud-usd-price-forecast-20-day-ema-remains-key-barrier-amid-firm-us-dollar-202609230542))
- **Oil rebounds on Iran diplomacy signals; gold slips on hawkish Fed**: WTI rose 1.8% to $92.16/bbl and Brent gained 3.9% to $103.08/bbl, snapping a five-day losing streak after President Trump called US-Iran talks "very good," even as Iran's President Pezeshkian told the UN General Assembly that Iran "will not surrender." Gold eased roughly 1-1.5% to around $4,280-$4,300/oz as the stronger flash PMI (58.4) and hawkish Fed tone outweighed safe-haven demand. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/23/iran-us-talks-crude-oil-un-wti.html), [Kitco](http://www.kitco.com/news/article/2026-09-23/spot-gold-drops-4280oz-flash-sp-composite-pmi-improves-584-september))

## Investment Implications
- The spike in the 10-year Treasury yield to its highest level since 2007 signals markets are repricing for a longer period of elevated rates, which weighed hardest on growth-sensitive names (Nasdaq, Russell 2000) and could keep pressure on equity valuations if yields continue climbing.
- Rising odds of an October Fed hike alongside a likely September 29 RBA hike suggest a synchronized global tightening bias is reasserting itself, which may keep both gold and rate-sensitive equities range-bound or under pressure near-term.
- Oil's rebound on Iran diplomacy optimism remains fragile given Iran's public defiance at the UN, so energy price direction is likely to stay highly sensitive to headline risk out of the ongoing talks.
