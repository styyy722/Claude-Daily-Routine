# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-10-02

## Executive Summary
- US equities rallied broadly (S&P 500 +0.73% to 7,722.72, Nasdaq Composite +1.19% to 27,190.86, Dow +0.49% to 51,176.96) after a much weaker-than-expected September jobs report cut October Fed rate-hike odds from ~64% to ~16%.
- The ASX 200 rebounded ~0.79% to ~8,682, clawing back part of Thursday's steep 1.99% drop, as bargain-hunters returned; AUD/USD firmed 0.41% to 0.6941.
- Oil fell sharply (WTI -1.78% to $91.22/bbl) after G7 nations signalled plans to release emergency reserves to ease fuel costs, while gold slipped (-0.85% to ~$4,142/oz) as the weak jobs data initially lifted, then failed to sustain, safe-haven demand.

## Key Financial News
- **Weak September jobs report reshapes Fed outlook**: US nonfarm payrolls rose just 29,000 versus ~90,000 expected (down from 133,000 in August), and the unemployment rate ticked up to 4.2% from 4.1%. Traders slashed October rate-hike odds from ~64% to ~16%, fuelling a broad equity rally. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-friday-october-2-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-september-jobs-report-080623878.html))
- **Nasdaq hits intraday record on AI/chip strength**: Nvidia rose over 2% to an intraday record of $237.87, briefly touching a ~$5.72 trillion market cap, powering the Nasdaq Composite to a fresh intraday high before closing +1.19%. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-oct-02-2026))
- **Tesla jumps on Q3 delivery beat**: Tesla gained roughly 4% after reporting Q3 deliveries of 486,532 vehicles, topping analyst estimates. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-oct-02-2026))
- **Nike slumps on revenue miss and layoffs**: Nike shares fell about 6% after fiscal Q1 revenue came in below expectations, with the company also announcing layoffs. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-oct-02-2026))
- **ASX 200 rebounds after steepest drop since March**: The index rose ~0.79% to ~8,682, recovering part of Thursday's 1.99% slide, as bargain-hunters stepped back in; AUD/USD firmed 0.41% to 0.6941 as markets continued digesting Tuesday's RBA rate hike to 4.60%. ([Trading Economics – Australia Stock Market](https://tradingeconomics.com/australia/stock-market))
- **Oil drops as G7 signals reserve releases**: WTI fell 1.78% to $91.22/bbl after G7 nations announced plans to release emergency oil reserves to help stabilise fuel costs; Brent, by contrast, ticked up 0.38% to $102.58/bbl, with sources showing some intraday divergence between the two benchmarks. ([Trading Economics – Crude Oil](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/crude-oil), [Trading Economics – Brent](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/brent-crude-oil))
- **Gold and iron ore ease**: Gold slipped 0.85% to around $4,142/oz, with Trading Economics noting the metal "traded around $4,140 an ounce on Friday" as the weak jobs data's initial safe-haven lift faded against the backdrop of surging equities; iron ore eased 0.81% to $91.35/T. ([Trading Economics – Gold](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/gold), [Trading Economics – Iron Ore](https://tradingeconomics.com/commodity/iron-ore))

## Investment Implications
- The sharp drop in October rate-hike odds (64% to 16%) on a single soft jobs report underscores how data-dependent and reactive current Fed pricing is; further labour-market data could swing expectations just as quickly in either direction.
- AI/chip-led strength (Nvidia) continues to be a dominant driver of US index gains, meaning broad market performance remains concentrated in a narrow set of mega-cap technology names.
- The ASX 200's rebound alongside a firming AUD/USD suggests Thursday's selloff may have been overdone on stock-specific (Lynas/Ampol) rather than macro factors, though RBA policy expectations remain a key swing factor for the local market.
