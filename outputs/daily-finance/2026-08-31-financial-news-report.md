# Daily Financial News Report – 2026-08-31

## Executive Summary
- US and Asia-Pacific equities fell Monday after the US struck Iranian rocket launchers near the Strait of Hormuz on Sunday, sending oil prices sharply higher and reviving inflation and Fed rate-hike bets.
- Despite Monday's pullback, all three major US indices still closed out August with solid monthly gains (S&P 500 +~2.5%, Nasdaq +3%+, Dow +~1.3%), and the 10-year Treasury yield briefly topped 4.75%, its highest since January 2025.
- China's official NBS Manufacturing PMI rose to 49.8 in August (from 49.2), beating the 49.7 forecast though still marking a second straight month of contraction; the Non-Manufacturing PMI held steady at 49.0.

## Key Financial News

- **US strikes Iranian rocket launchers near Strait of Hormuz:** US forces hit two Iranian launchers on Larak Island on Sunday after the IRGC was observed preparing to send mines into the strait — the first US strike in the region in about a month. Iran's Revolutionary Guards said they retaliated by targeting US bases in Jordan and the UAE. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/30/us-iran-strikes-strait-hormuz.html), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-30/us-strikes-iranian-rocket-launchers-in-first-attack-in-weeks))
- **Oil surges on renewed Middle East risk:** Brent crude jumped roughly 3% to above $88-91/bbl and WTI rose to around $86/bbl as traders priced in fresh supply risk through the Strait of Hormuz, a key chokepoint for global crude and LNG flows. ([Boston Globe](https://www.bostonglobe.com/2026/08/31/business/asian-shares-us-futures-retreat-oil-prices-surge-after-us-strike-on-iran/), [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/31/gas-prices-oil-us-iran-strikes-strait-hormuz/))
- **US equities close lower but post a winning August:** The S&P 500 fell 0.33% to 7,686.14, the Dow dropped 0.70% (-374 points) to 53,185.90, and the Nasdaq Composite slipped 0.12% to 26,370.89 — but all three still finished August solidly higher (S&P +~2.5%, Nasdaq +3%+, Dow +~1.3%), extending a multi-month winning streak. Energy stocks were a bright spot, with Exxon Mobil and Chevron both gaining as oil rallied. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-august-31-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-113851714.html))
- **Fed rate-hike odds jump further on inflation risk:** September rate-hike odds were repriced to roughly 62% (from ~40% a week earlier) as the spike in oil prices reinforced the hawkish narrative from Fed Chair Warsh's Jackson Hole speech. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-august-31-dow-sp-500-nasdaq-113851714.html))
- **10-year Treasury yield tops 4.75%:** The 10-year yield briefly breached 4.75% intraday, its highest level since January 2025, before settling around 4.72%, as the combination of oil-driven inflation risk and hawkish Fed repricing pushed yields higher across the curve; the 2-year sat near 4.34% and the 30-year near 5.20%. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-31/treasury-10-year-yield-tops-4-75-highest-since-january-2025))
- **China PMI beats forecast but stays in contraction:** The NBS Manufacturing PMI rose to 49.8 in August from July's 49.2, above the 49.7 consensus, with output and new orders (including new export orders) returning to expansion even as employment stayed subdued; the Non-Manufacturing PMI held at 49.0. ([FXStreet](https://www.fxstreet.com/news/chinas-nbs-manufacturing-pmi-climbs-to-498-in-august-non-manufacturing-pmi-steadies-at-490-202608310132))
- **ASX and Asia-Pacific markets slip on risk-off tone:** The ASX 200 closed down around 0.2% to roughly 9,096, tracking a broadly negative regional and Wall Street futures tone as investors weighed the Iran escalation against the improved China PMI print. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-08-31/asx-markets-business-live-news-wall-street-slides/107095734))
- **Gold slips despite geopolitical risk:** Bullion eased to roughly $4,443-4,483/oz, down from Friday's close near $4,598.89, as broad dollar strength from the hawkish Fed repricing outweighed the usual safe-haven bid from Middle East tensions. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-prices-today-monday-august-31-2026-gold-sinks-following-us-strikes-on-iran-123744944.html))

## Investment Implications

- The combination of an oil-price shock and a rising Fed rate-hike probability (now ~62% for September) raises stagflation-style risk for rate-sensitive assets; equity volatility is likely to stay elevated into the 15-16 September FOMC meeting.
- Energy-sector equities and inflation-protected assets may continue to outperform broader indices if the Strait of Hormuz situation escalates further, while long-duration growth stocks and gold remain vulnerable to further dollar strength.
- China's PMI beat, while still in contraction, offers a modest offset to global growth concerns and could support AUD and Australian resources names if the improvement extends into September data, though near-term risk sentiment is currently dominated by the Iran conflict.
