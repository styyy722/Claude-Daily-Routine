## Key Financial News
- **Middle East escalation**: President Trump declared the recent ceasefire "over" and said he was reinstating a blockade on Iranian shipping through the Strait of Hormuz, including a proposed 20% fee on transiting cargo, after US strikes near the strait prompted Iranian retaliation against US allies in Kuwait, Jordan and Qatar. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-july-13-dow-sp-nasdaq-113249278.html), [Al Jazeera](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/7/13/oil-prices-jump-as-us-and-iran-trade-attacks-over-strait-of-hormuz))
- **US equities**: The S&P 500 (-0.79%) and Nasdaq Composite (-1.55%) fell as chip stocks tumbled; the Dow (-0.26%) held up better as energy shares rallied on higher oil prices. Russell 2000 small caps fell about 0.5%. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/live/stock-market-today-monday-july-13-dow-sp-nasdaq-113249278.html))
- **Semiconductors**: SK Hynix's US-listed shares dropped 9% following its Friday Nasdaq debut; Micron fell 4%, Sandisk shed 12%, AMD slipped 4% and Intel pulled back 6% amid renewed AI-chip demand pessimism. ([U.S. News](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-07-13/s-p-500-nasdaq-futures-decline-as-us-iran-escalation-rattles-sentiment))

([MarketIndex](https://www.marketindex.com.au/news/asx-200-live-today-monday-13th-july))
- **Central banks / rates**: New Fed Chair Kevin Warsh is due to give his first congressional testimony this week (House Tuesday, Senate later in the week), with markets looking for signals on the rate path following the June rate hold. ([Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-07-11/warsh-and-us-inflation-will-set-tone-for-july-fed-decision))

([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/13/gold-slides-over-1percent-as-oil-surges-on-strait-of-hormuz-closure-fears.html))

Oil jumped roughly 3% (WTI near $74/bbl; Brent above $82) on Strait of Hormuz supply-disruption fears, while gold fell 1.4% for a second straight session as the higher-for-longer rate view outweighed haven demand. Iron ore in China eased slightly (-0.93%) but remains off recent lows amid a planned strike at BHP's Port Hedland terminal on 16 July. ## Investment Implications
- Escalating Middle East conflict and the threat of a Strait of Hormuz blockade is a fresh source of energy-driven inflation risk, complicating the path for rate cuts just as a new Fed chair takes the podium.
- The pullback in high-flying semiconductor names suggests AI-trade positioning remains sensitive to negative catalysts, even as broader indices had been near highs.
- Energy stocks' resilience highlights a rotation dynamic investors may watch if oil-driven geopolitical risk persists, while safe-haven flows (gold, bonds) are currently being overridden by inflation/rate concerns.
