## Key Financial News
- **US stocks fall for a third day**: The S&P 500 lost 0.48% to 7,636.36, the Dow dropped 405.41 points (-0.77%) to 52,380.66, and the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.64% to 26,253.34, as rising Treasury yields and oil prices continued to rattle investors. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/stock-market-today-live-updates.html))
- **Treasury yields hit multi-year high**: The 10-year Treasury yield jumped to 4.857% — its highest level since November 2023 — after the Treasury Department announced it is tripling its buyback operation of longer-dated government debt to $6 billion. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/stock-market-today-live-updates.html))
- **US-Iran conflict escalates sharply**: The US military struck five Iranian oil tankers after Iran fired ballistic missiles at a US Navy warship, with reports of explosions near Iran's Kharg Island and Iran-backed Houthi attacks on Saudi energy facilities. ([TheStreet](https://www.thestreet.com/stock-market-today/stock-market-today-dow-jones-sp-500-nasdaq-updates-sept-09-2026); [ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-09/asx-markets-business-live-news/106752628))
- **Oil surges toward $100/bbl**: Brent crude rose as high as $99.17–$102.05/bbl (up roughly 1.3–2.9% on the day) while WTI traded around $96.79/bbl, driven by the escalating Middle East conflict; the Fed rate-hike probability for the September 15–16 meeting rose to 58% as higher oil fed inflation concerns. ([Fortune](https://fortune.com/article/price-of-oil-09-09-2026/); [ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-09/asx-markets-business-live-news/106752628))
- **ASX 200 hits fresh six-week low**: The index closed at 8,911.4, down 9.4 points (-0.11%); gold miners Westgold, Evolution Mining, Resolute Mining, Kingsgate Consolidated and Northern Star fell 3–6.5% while energy and resources names including BHP and Woodside Energy gained on the oil rally. Minerals 260 (+10.37%) and Austal (+7.13%) led gainers; SEEK (-5.60%) and PEXA Group (-5.5%) lagged. ([ABC News](https://www.abc.net.au/news/2026-09-09/asx-markets-business-live-news/106752628))
- **Gold whipsaws on Iran headlines**: Gold December futures opened at $4,399/oz, down 0.9% from Tuesday's close, after the US strikes on Iranian tankers, before recovering to roughly $4,438/oz in early trading as safe-haven demand returned. ([Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/personal-finance/investing/article/gold-price-today-wednesday-september-9-2026-gold-prices-drop-following-us-strikes-on-five-iranian-oil-tankers-104123795.html))
- **Wall Street strategists stay constructive**: JPMorgan's Mislav Matejka said pullback risk is lessening as the market broadens out, with equity indexes holding near record highs even as momentum has faltered, and expects a broadening of performance leadership in the second half of the year. ([CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/08/stock-market-today-live-updates.html))

## Investment Implications
- The simultaneous jump in Treasury yields and oil prices is squeezing risk assets from two sides at once, and now directly threatens to tip the Fed toward a hike rather than a cut at its September 15–16 meeting.
- The US-Iran naval escalation raises the tail risk of a more sustained oil-supply shock; markets have so far treated it as a headline risk rather than a structural one, but that could change quickly if disruption to Strait of Hormuz shipping materializes.
- The ASX's split reaction — gold miners sold off while energy/resources rallied — shows the market currently reading the oil spike primarily as a rates/inflation risk rather than a broad-based safe-haven trigger.
